Title: Hemp as a potential raw material toward a sustainable world: A review
Authors: A T M Faiz Ahmed, Md Zahidul Islam, Md Sultan Mahmud, Md Emdad Sarker, Md Reajul Islam
Year: 2022
Venue: Heliyon
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2022.e08753
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4205397448

Abstract:
L. (hemp) has received a lot of attention because of its multipurpose usability, short production cycle, low capital demand in cultivation, possibility of carbon-negative transformation and easy carbon sequestering material. This paper reviews hemp as a very promising renewable resource including its potential uses in paper, textiles, composites, biofuel, and food industry.
