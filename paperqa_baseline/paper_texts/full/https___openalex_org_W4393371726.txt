Title: Cross-border destination image for sustainable tourism development in peripheral areas
Authors: Belén Maldonado-López, Pablo Ledesma‐Chaves, Eloy Gil‐Cordero
Year: 2024
Venue: Journal of Travel & Tourism Marketing
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/10548408.2024.2319864
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4393371726

Abstract:
The objective of this study is to provide an analysis of the processes involved in the&#13;\nconstruction of the perception of a cross-border sustainable tourism destination image in&#13;\nperipheral regions. The research presented was conducted using an empirical approach that&#13;\nmerges qualitative and quantitative techniques. The main results of this study include the&#13;\nidentification of the key components that contribute to the formation of the image of&#13;\na sustainable cross-border tourist destination in the region encompassing Alcoutim&#13;\n(Portugal) and Sanlúcar de Guadiana (Spain).
