Title: Techno-economic and environmental impacts assessments of sustainable aviation fuel production from forest residues
Authors: Jayendra P. Ahire, Richard Bergman, Troy Runge, Seyed Hashem Mousavi‐Avval, Debangsu Bhattacharyya, Tristan R. Brown, Jack Wang
Year: 2024
Venue: Sustainable Energy & Fuels
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4se00749b
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4401535492

Abstract:
Sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) from forest residues is a promising pathway to reduce aviation's carbon footprint. This study assesses the techno-economic and environmental impacts of producing SAF via Fischer–Tropsch synthesis, with soil carbon benefits and greenhouse gas reductions.
