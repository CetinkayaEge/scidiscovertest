Title: Mitochondrial metabolism sustains CD8+ T cell migration for an efficient infiltration into solid tumors
Authors: Luca Simula, Mattia Fumagalli, Lene Vimeux, Irena Rajnpreht, Philippe Icard, Gary Birsen, Dongjie An, Frédéric Pendino, Adrien Rouault, Nadège Bercovici, Diane Damotte, Audrey Mansuet‐Lupo, Marco Alifano, Marie‐Clotilde Alves‐Guerra, Emmanuel Donnadieu
Year: 2024
Venue: Nature Communications
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46377-7
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4392639129

Abstract:
T cell intratumoral migration and CAR T cell recruitment into tumor islets leading to better control of tumor growth in human xenograft models. Our study highlights the rationale of targeting mitochondrial metabolism to enhance the migration and antitumor efficacy of CAR T cells in treating solid tumors.
