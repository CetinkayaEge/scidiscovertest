Title: The 12 principles of green membrane materials and processes for realizing the United Nations' sustainable development goals
Authors: György Székely
Year: 2024
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4su00027g
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4392060582

Abstract:
The 12 principles of green membrane materials and processes are formulated. Their contributions to the 3 pillars of sustainability and the UN-SDGs are explained. Research priorities are set. A membrane waste management hierarchy is introduced.
