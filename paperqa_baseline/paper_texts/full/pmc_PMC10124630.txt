Title: Burkholderia pseudomallei Laboratory Exposure, Arizona, USA
Authors: Lisa J. Speiser, Erin H. Graf, Maria Teresa Seville, Kai Singbartl, Mary L. Dalton, Denise Harrington, Melissa Kretschmer, Marina Kuljanin, Karen Zabel, Rebecca Sunenshine, Irene Ruberto, Heather Venkat, Thomas E. Grys
Year: 2023
Venue: Emerging Infectious Diseases
DOI: 10.3201/eid2905.221865
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10124630

Abstract:
We describe an incidental Burkholderia pseudomallei laboratory exposure in Arizona, USA. Because melioidosis cases are increasing in the United States and B. pseudomallei reservoirs have been discovered in the Gulf Coast Region, US laboratory staff could be at increased risk for B. pseudomallei exposure.
