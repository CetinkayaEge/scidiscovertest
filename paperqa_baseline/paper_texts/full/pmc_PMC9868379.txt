Title: COVID-19 vaccines versus pediatric hospitalization
Authors: Jaime S. Rosa Duque, Daniel Leung, Ka Man Yip, Derek H.L. Lee, Hung-kwan So, Wilfred H.S. Wong, Yu Lung Lau
Year: 2023
Venue: Cell Reports Medicine
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.100936
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9868379

Abstract:
Summary Vaccine effectiveness of BNT162b2 and CoronaVac against COVID-19-associated hospitalization and moderate-to-severe disease due to SARS-CoV-2 Omicron BA.2 is studied from the 1.36 million doses administered to 766,601 of 953,400 children aged 3–11 years and adolescents aged 12–18 years in Hong Kong as of April 2022. These vaccines confer substantial protection.
