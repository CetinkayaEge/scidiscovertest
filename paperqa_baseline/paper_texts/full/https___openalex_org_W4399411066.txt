Title: The intersection of renewable energy and environmental health: Advancements in sustainable solutions
Authors: Darlington Eze Ekechukwu, Peter Simpa
Year: 2024
Venue: International Journal of Applied Research in Social Sciences
DOI: https://doi.org/10.51594/ijarss.v6i6.1175
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4399411066

Abstract:
The intersection of renewable energy and environmental health represents a critical nexus where advancements in sustainable solutions hold significant promise for addressing pressing global challenges. This review explores the intricate relationship between renewable energy technologies and their impact on environmental health, highlighting the urgent need for transformative initiatives. By examining the adverse effects of traditional energy sources on air quality, water purity, and land integrity, it becomes evident that the transition to renewable energy is imperative for mitigating these environmental health risks. Through case studies and technological innovations, this review showcases the tangible benefits of integrating renewable energy systems, including reductions in air pollution-related illnesses, improvements in water sanitation, and the mitigation of land degradation. However, despite the numerous advantages, challenges such as economic barriers, technological limitations, and policy obstacles hinder widespread adoption. Overcoming these challenges requires collaborative efforts across sectors and innovative approaches to research and development. By prioritizing investments in renewable energy infrastructure, implementing supportive policies, and fostering international cooperation, society can pave the way for a healthier and more sustainable future. This review underscores the critical importance of advancing sustainable solutions at the intersection of renewable energy and environmental health, emphasizing the urgency of collective action to address the complex challenges facing our planet. Keywords: Renewable Energy, Environmental Health, Sustainable Solutions, Advancements, Technological Innovations.
