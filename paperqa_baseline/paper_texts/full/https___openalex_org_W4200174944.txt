Title: Application of carotenoids in sustainable energy and green electronics
Authors: Ajay Singh, Trinetra Mukherjee
Year: 2021
Venue: Materials Advances
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1ma01070k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4200174944

Abstract:
Carotenoids are natural pigments responsible for coloring in flowers and seeds. The semiconducting nature of carotenoids makes them a suitable candidate for solar harvesting and biodegradable nanoelectronic devices.
