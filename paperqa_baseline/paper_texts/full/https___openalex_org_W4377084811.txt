Title: Bioethanol, internal combustion engines and the development of zero-waste biorefineries: an approach towards sustainable motor spirit
Authors: Shaswat Barua, Debojeet Sahu, Firdous Sultana, Swagata Baruah, Sadhan Mahapatra
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3su00080j
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4377084811

Abstract:
This review article describes the generations of bioethanol and their sustainability. Furthermore, the compatibility of bioethanol blended fuels with motor engines is described. Also, approaches towards zero-waste biorefineries are highlighted.
