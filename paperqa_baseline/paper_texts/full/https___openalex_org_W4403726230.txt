Title: Regenerative Agriculture for Sustainable Food Systems
Authors: Kumar, Sandeep, Meena, Ram Swaroop, Sheoran, Parvender, Jhariya, Manoj Kumar
Year: 2024
Venue: 
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-97-6691-8
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4403726230

Abstract:
Regenerative agriculture represents a paradigm shift, moving away from conventional, extractive farming methods towards practices that restore and enhance the natural systems on which agriculture depends.Regenerative Agriculture for Sustainable Food System is more than just a book for better farming techniques; it is a vision for a healthier planet and a sustainable future.We can create a food system that nourishes people and the environment by fostering a deeper connection between agriculture and the natural world.This book explores the potential of regenerative agriculture to address pressing issues such as soil degradation, biodiversity loss, and the impacts of climate change on farming systems.It is a comprehensive guide that combines scientific research, case studies, and practical insights to provide a roadmap for transitioning to more sustainable farming methods.This book covers the regenerative agriculture and emphasizes the importance of soil health, biodiversity, and ecological balance, recognizing that healthy soils are the foundation of a productive and resilient agricultural system.By focusing on practices that rebuild soil organic matter, enhance soil structure, and promote soil biological activity, regenerative agriculture improves crop yields and sequesters carbon, helping to mitigate climate change.Farmers, policymakers, and researchers must navigate a complex economic, social, and environmental landscape.This book addresses these challenges head-on, providing practical solutions and highlighting the progress and opportunities ahead.One of the key themes of this book is the integration of regenerative practices with the broader goals of sustainable development.By aligning agricultural practices with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, regenerative agriculture can contribute to achieving food security, promoting sustainable economic growth, and fostering resilient communities.This book delves into the complexities and nuances of regenerative practices, offering readers a detailed understanding of how these methods can be implemented across different agricultural contexts.The chapters cover various topics, from nutrient management and crop rotation to agroforestry and biological pest control, each contributing to a comprehensive understanding of regenerative practices.As we face the dual crises of climate change and food insecurity, adopting regenerative agriculture presents a hopeful path forward.This book aims to equip farmers, researchers, policymakers, and educators with the knowledge and tools to implement regenerative practices effectively.It is a call to action for a collective effort to transform our agricultural systems, ensuring a sustainable and resilient future for vi generations to come.This book is an invitation to join a growing movement dedicated to our lands' regeneration, our communities' revitalization, and the restoration of our planet.Many people can benefit from this book, including teachers, researchers, scientists, government planners, policymakers, and students of the agricultural sciences.In addition, this book is relevant to climate change scientists, extension workers, and capacity builders nationally and internationally.
