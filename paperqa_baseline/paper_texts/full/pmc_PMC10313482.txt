Title: Sudden Cardiac Arrest in the Postpartum Period Due to Long QT Syndrome and Dilated Cardiomyopathy
Authors: Daniel R. Patterson, Jonathan A. Pan, Nisha Hosadurg, Mohamed Morsy
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101882
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313482

Abstract:
We describe the case of a previously healthy patient presenting with sudden cardiac arrest in the postpartum period as a result of concomitant congenital type 1 long QT syndrome and BAG3 dilated cardiomyopathy. This case highlights the increased rate of cardiac events for patients with long QT syndrome in the postpartum period. ( Level of Difficulty: Advanced. ) Central Illustration
