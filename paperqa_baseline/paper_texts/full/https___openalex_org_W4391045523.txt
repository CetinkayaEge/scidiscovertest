Title: INNOVATIVE APPROACHES TO WASTE RESOURCE MANAGEMENT: IMPLICATIONS FOR ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND POLICY
Authors: Michael Ayorinde Dada, Alexander Obaigbena, Michael Tega Majemite, Johnson Sunday Oliha, Preye Winston Biu
Year: 2024
Venue: Engineering Science & Technology Journal
DOI: https://doi.org/10.51594/estj.v5i1.731
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4391045523

Abstract:
This research explores innovative approaches to waste resource management and their implications for environmental sustainability and policy development. Emphasizing Circular Economy principles, technological innovations, and community engagement, the study delves into the challenges and opportunities presented by these strategies. The policy implications underscore the need for adaptive frameworks, aligning regulations with socio-economic contexts. While technological advancements hold promise, economic viability, market demand, and societal behaviours pose challenges. Despite these hurdles, embracing innovative waste management practices offers transformative potential, fostering resource conservation, pollution mitigation, and climate change resilience. This research contributes insights for policymakers, industry stakeholders, and communities navigating the dynamic landscape of waste resource management. Keywords: Waste Resource Management, Circular Economy, Technological Innovations, Environmental Sustainability, Policy Implications.
