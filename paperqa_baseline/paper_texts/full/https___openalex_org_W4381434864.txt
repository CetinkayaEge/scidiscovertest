Title: Nanoarchitectonics of metal oxide materials for sustainable technologies and environmental applications
Authors: Davide Barreca, Chiara Maccato
Year: 2023
Venue: CrystEngComm
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3ce00431g
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4381434864

Abstract:
This overview, summarizing recent progresses in the fabrication of multi-functional oxide nanomaterials for sustainable and environmental end-uses, sheds light on the interplay between their chemico-physical features and functional properties.
