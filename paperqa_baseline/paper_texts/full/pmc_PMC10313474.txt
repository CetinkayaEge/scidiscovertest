Title: Staged Acclimatization in a Failing Fontan by AFR in AFR
Authors: Jenny E. Zablah, Natalie Soszyn, Gareth J. Morgan
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101868
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313474

Abstract:
We describe serial implantation of atrial flow regulator (AFR) devices in the Fontan fenestration of a 4-year-old patient. Initially, the fenestration size was decreased using a 6/5 AFR, resulting in improved saturations and hemodynamics. One year later, further improvement was achieved by placing a 4/10 AFR inside the original device. ( Level of Difficulty: Advanced. ) Central Illustration
