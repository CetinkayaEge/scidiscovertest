Title: A breath of deadly air
Authors: Michael Gross
Year: 2021
Venue: Current Biology
DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2021.02.002
Paper ID: pmc_PMC8011655

Abstract:
Air pollution kills many more people every year than COVID-19 has in its first year but is receiving less attention. India is facing a pollution crisis from multiple sources, while European cities have mainly their motor vehicles to blame. Mounting evidence suggests that particle pollution may harm every organ of the body. Michael Gross reports.
