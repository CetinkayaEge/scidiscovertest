Title: Sustainable solvent selection for the manufacture of methylammonium lead triiodide (MAPbI3) perovskite solar cells
Authors: Alexander Doolin, Rhys Charles, Catherine S. P. De Castro, Rodrigo García‐Rodríguez, Emmanuel V. Péan, Rahul Patidar, Tom Dunlop, Cécile Charbonneau, Trystan Watson, Matthew L. Davies
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc00079a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3137560241

Abstract:
An approach for screening solvent systems for PSCs to substitute REACH restricted DMF with no loss in device performance.
