Title: Safety and Efficacy Associated With a Family-Centered Procedural Sedation Protocol for Children With Autism Spectrum Disorder or Developmental Delay
Authors: Cyril Sahyoun, Baruch Krauss, Martina Bevacqua, Artiom Antonsen, Laurent Jardinier, Egidio Barbi
Year: 2023
Venue: JAMA Network Open
DOI: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.15974
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10230313

Abstract:
This case series describes a family-centered procedural sedation protocol including home desensitization to intranasal drug delivery, environmental modification, and intranasal dexmedetomidine combined with nitrous oxide for children with autism spectrum disorder or developmental delay.
