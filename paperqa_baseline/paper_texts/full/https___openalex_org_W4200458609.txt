Title: Breastfeeding Support Rooms and Their Contribution to Sustainable Development Goals: A Qualitative Study
Authors: Carolina Belomo de Souza, Sônia Isoyama Venâncio, Regina Paula Guimarães Vieira Cavalcante da Silva
Year: 2021
Venue: Frontiers in Public Health
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.732061
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4200458609

Abstract:
In this novel study, we demonstrate how, from a female point of view, breastfeeding support rooms can contribute to 8 of the 17 sustainable development goals and should therefore be encouraged and promoted.
