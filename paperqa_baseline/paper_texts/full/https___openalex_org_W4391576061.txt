Title: The 2023 Kahramanmaraş Earthquake Sequence: finding a path to a more resilient, sustainable, and equitable society
Authors: Carmine Galasso, Eyitayo A. Opabola
Year: 2024
Venue: Communications Engineering
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s44172-024-00170-y
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4391576061

Abstract:
Learning from the 2023 Kahramanmaraş Earthquake Sequence offers valuable insights into disaster recovery. Here we delve into the intricacies of the “Build Back Better” (BBB) concept, underscoring the importance of recovery and reconstruction efforts toward a future that is not only more resilient but also more sustainable and equitable.
