Title: Green technologies and sustainability: A new trend
Authors: Jinsong Wu, Vladimir Strezov
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Technologies and Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.grets.2023.100008
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4321230336

Abstract:
This paper provides a view of green technologies with the relations to sustainability. This paper also discusses the needs of the new international journal, Green Technologies and Sustainability (GTS).
