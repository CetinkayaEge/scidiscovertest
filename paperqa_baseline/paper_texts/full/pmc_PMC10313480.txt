Title: Survival Into the Seventh Decade of Life Following Mustard Repair
Authors: Matthew Peters, Steven Port, Heather Sanders, Nasir Sulemanjee, A. Jamil Tajik
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101885
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313480

Abstract:
Atrial switch procedures (Senning and Mustard) for transposition of the great arteries have largely been abandoned for arterial switch procedures. The number of surviving patients who have undergone atrial switch procedures is declining. We present a case of the oldest known survivor (aged 67 years) of the Mustard procedure.
