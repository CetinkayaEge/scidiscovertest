Title: Emerging Invasive Group A Streptococcus M1 UK Lineage Detected by Allele-Specific PCR, England, 2020 1
Authors: Xiangyun Zhi, Ho Kwong Li, Hanqi Li, Zuzanna Loboda, Samson Charles, Ana Vieira, Kristin Huse, Elita Jauneikaite, Lucy Reeves, Kai Yi Mok, Juliana Coelho, Theresa Lamagni, Shiranee Sriskandan
Year: 2023
Venue: Emerging Infectious Diseases
DOI: 10.3201/eid2905.221887
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10124639

Abstract:
Increasing reports of invasive Streptococcus pyogenes infections mandate surveillance for toxigenic lineage M1 UK . An allele-specific PCR was developed to distinguish M1 UK from other emm 1 strains. The M1 UK lineage represented 91% of invasive emm 1 isolates in England in 2020. Allele-specific PCR will permit surveillance for M1 UK without need for genome sequencing.
