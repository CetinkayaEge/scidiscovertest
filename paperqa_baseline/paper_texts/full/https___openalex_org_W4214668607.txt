Title: Disconnection: How Measured Separations From Journalistic Norms and Labor Can Help Sustain Journalism
Authors: Valérie Bélair‐Gagnon, Diana Bossio, Avery E. Holton, Logan Molyneux
Year: 2022
Venue: Social Media + Society
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/20563051221077217
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4214668607

Abstract:
This commentary considers the concept of “disconnection” as a way to understand practices of contemporary digital journalism while advocating for consideration of disconnection as a necessary component of sustainable journalism.
