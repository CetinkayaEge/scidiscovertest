Title: Digital light processing (DLP) 3D-fabricated antimicrobial hydrogel with a sustainable resin of methacrylated woody polysaccharides and hybrid silver-lignin nanospheres
Authors: Luyao Wang, Qingbo Wang, Anna Slita, Oskar Backman, Zahra Gounani, Emil Rosqvist, Jouko Peltonen, Stefan Willför, Chunlin Xu, Jessica M. Rosenholm, Xiaoju Wang
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc03841a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4211049562

Abstract:
Efficient antimicrobial hydrogels are fabricated by digital light processing (DLP) printing out of a sustainable resin, which is formulated with methacrylated O -acetyl-galactoglucomannan and lignin nanospheres decorated with silver nanoparticles.
