Title: Biochar implications in cleaner agricultural production and environmental sustainability
Authors: Subhash Babu, Raghavendra Singh, Sanjeev Kumar, Sanjay Singh Rathore, Devideen Yadav, S. K. Yadav, Vivek Yadav, M. A. Ansari, Anup Das, G. A. Rajanna, Owais Ali Wani, Rishi Raj, Dinesh Kumar Yadav, Vinod Kumar Singh
Year: 2023
Venue: Environmental Science Advances
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2va00324d
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4379051779

Abstract:
Biochar production and utilization is proposed as an innovative approach for enhancing food production and climate change mitigation. Globally, biochar has the potential to increase crop productivity by ∼11% and reduces annual human-induced GHG emissions by ∼12%.
