Title: Racial disparity in curative treatment and survival from solid-organ cancers
Authors: S K Kamarajah, P Sylla, S R Markar
Year: 2021
Venue: The British Journal of Surgery
DOI: 10.1093/bjs/znab089
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10364912

Abstract:
Race is an important prognostic factor affecting allocation to intervention and survival from cancer treatment in the USA. The mechanism of this association is an important area for future investigation.
