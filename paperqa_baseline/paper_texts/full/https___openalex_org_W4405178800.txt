Title: RETRACTED: Optimizing hydrogen integration in vehicle fuel systems for sustainable development: A step towards economic decarbonization
Authors: Muhammad Khalid Anser, Sajid Ali, Muhammad Umair, Rubab Javid, Muhammad Tayab
Year: 2024
Venue: International Journal of Hydrogen Energy
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2024.12.009
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4405178800

Abstract:
This article has been retracted: please see Elsevier Policy on Article Withdrawal ( https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies/article-withdrawal ). This article has been retracted at the request of the Editor-in-Chief. 50% of the references in the paper belong to a single author. The excessive citation of these sources raises significant questions about the integrity of the literature review process and undermines the novelty and rigor of the claims made in the paper. Furthermore, the journal noted that only Muhammad Tayyab was listed as the author in the original manuscript, while authors Muhammad Khalid Anser, Sajid Ali, Muhammad Umair and Rubab Javid were added to the author list during the revision without the editor’s approval, which is a breach of journal policy.
