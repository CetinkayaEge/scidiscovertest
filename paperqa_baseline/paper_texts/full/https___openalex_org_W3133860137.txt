Title: Sustainable plasma-catalytic bubbles for hydrogen peroxide synthesis
Authors: Renwu Zhou, Tianqi Zhang, Rusen Zhou, Sen Wang, Danhua Mei, Anne Mai‐Prochnow, Janith Weerasinghe, Zhi Fang, Kostya Ostrikov, Patrick J. Cullen
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc00198a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3133860137

Abstract:
This work demonstrates the plasma-bubble as an energy efficient and sustainable approach for green and decentralised H2O2production.
