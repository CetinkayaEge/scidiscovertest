Title: Eco-friendly, sustainable, and safe energy storage: a nature-inspired materials paradigm shift
Authors: Thiago Bertaglia, Carlos M. Costa, S. Lanceros‐Méndez, Frank N. Crespilho
Year: 2024
Venue: Materials Advances
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ma00363b
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4401370914

Abstract:
This work demonstrates how nature-inspired and derived approaches can significantly impact energy storage. Also, it provides a workflow to accelerate the development of these technologies, helping these batteries move closer to market readiness.
