Title: Progresses and future prospects in biodegradation of marine biopolymers and emerging biopolymer-based materials for sustainable marine ecosystems
Authors: François Samalens, Martin Thomas, Marion Claverie, Natalia Castejón, Yi Zhang, Thierry Pigot, Sylvie Blanc, Susana C. M. Fernandes
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc04327g
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4210436962

Abstract:
With approximately 250 000 marine species, the ocean is a vast reservoir of biodiversity and an abundant biological resource of natural polymers and microorganisms producing biopolymer-degrading enzymes.
