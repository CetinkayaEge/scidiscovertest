Title: Status and gaps toward fossil-free sustainable chemical production
Authors: Gabriele Centi, Siglinda Perathoner
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2gc01572b
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4292949496

Abstract:
Chemical industry needs and gaps regarding its transformation to fossil-free production to meet the targets for net-zero emissions by the year 2050 are presented.
