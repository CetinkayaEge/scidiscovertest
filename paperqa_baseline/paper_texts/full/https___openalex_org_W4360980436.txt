Title: Inter-organisational Cooperation Oriented Towards Sustainability Involving SMEs: a Systematic Literature Review
Authors: Nathalia Suchek, Mário Franco
Year: 2023
Venue: Journal of the Knowledge Economy
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s13132-023-01196-x
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4360980436

As a result, the articles were organised into four groups, namely (1) cooperation for sustainability promoted by government initiatives, (2) effects of inter-organisational cooperation for sustainability, (3) process of cooperation oriented towards sustainability, and (4) start of discussions on cooperation for the circular economy. A framework is presented with an overview of the evolution of the field, highlighting the main factors and outcomes related to inter-organisational cooperation involving SMEs for sustainability. The review provides theoretical implications as future research avenues for academics and scholars as well as practical implications for entrepreneurs, managers and policy-makers.
