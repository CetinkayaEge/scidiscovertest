Title: Green hydrogen production for sustainable development: a critical examination of barriers and strategic opportunities
Authors: Juan Gabriel Segovia-Hernandez, Salvador Hernández, Enrique Cossío-Vargas, Maricruz Juárez-García, Eduardo Sánchez-Ramírez
Year: 2024
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4su00630e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4404760887

Abstract:
As the world endeavors to meet ambitious climate targets and mitigate carbon emissions, green hydrogen stands out as a versatile and scalable solution offering a viable pathway toward sustainable development.
