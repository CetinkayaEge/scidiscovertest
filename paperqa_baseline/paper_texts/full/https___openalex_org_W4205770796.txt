Title: Assessing International Sport Federations' Sustainability Practices: Toward Integrating Sustainability in Their Main Sports Events
Authors: Philjoo Moon, Emmanuel Bayle, Aurélien François
Year: 2022
Venue: Frontiers in Sports and Active Living
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2021.752085
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4205770796

Abstract:
In terms of theory, examining our data through the lens of neo-institutional theory provides insights into international federations' recent sustainability behaviors. Our findings enabled us to draw up a "sustainability ladder" of sport federations' responsibilities, which can be used to assess the degree to which they have embraced sustainability. In practical terms, our findings should encourage more sport federations to take concrete steps to improve their sustainability by implementing the five approaches.
