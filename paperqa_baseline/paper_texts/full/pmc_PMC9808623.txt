Title: Fast and accurate mapping of long reads to complete genome assemblies with VerityMap
Authors: Andrey V. Bzikadze, Alla Mikheenko, Pavel A. Pevzner
Year: 2022
Venue: Genome Research
DOI: 10.1101/gr.276871.122
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9808623

Abstract:
Recent advancements in long-read sequencing have enabled the telomere-to-telomere (complete) assembly of a human genome and are now contributing to the haplotype-resolved complete assemblies of multiple human genomes. Because the accuracy of read mapping tools deteriorates in highly repetitive regions, there is a need to develop accurate, error - exposing (detecting potential assembly errors), and diploid - awar e (distinguishing different haplotypes) tools for read mapping in complete assemblies. We describe the first accurate, error-exposing, and partially diploid-aware VerityMap tool for long-read mapping to complete assemblies.
