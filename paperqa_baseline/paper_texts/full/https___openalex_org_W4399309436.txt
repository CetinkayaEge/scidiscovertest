Title: Towards greener batteries: sustainable components and materials for next-generation batteries
Authors: Palanivel Molaiyan, Shubhankar Bhattacharyya, Glaydson S. dos Reis, Rafal Sliz, Andrea Paolella, Ulla Lassi
Year: 2024
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc05027k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4399309436

Abstract:
Greener batteries are an emerging approach for sustainable environments and a promising technology for future batteries. In this review, we discuss the main issues to fully achieve the potential of batteries with greener approaches.
