Title: Drugs that slow the progression of diabetic kidney disease: are renoprotective effects attenuated in heart failure?
Authors: Milton Packer, Tariq Jamal Siddiqi, Javed Butler
Year: 2023
Venue: European Heart Journal
DOI: 10.1093/eurheartj/ehad158
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10149530

Abstract:
Graphical Abstract Graphical Abstract Top panel: meta-analysis (random effects model) for occurrence of end-stage kidney disease in four major outcomes trials of SGLT2 inhibitors in heart failure. Bottom panel: depiction of different mechanisms driving the progression of chronic kidney disease in type 2 diabetes, as compared with chronic heart failure. Drugs that lower intraglomerular pressures may be beneficial in type 2 diabetes (characterized by glomerular hyperfiltration), but detrimental in chronic heart failure (characterized by glomerular hypofiltration).
