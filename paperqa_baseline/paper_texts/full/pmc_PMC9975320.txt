Title: Mesencephalic locomotor region stimulation—cuneiform or pedunculopontine?
Authors: Emily R. Burnside, Frank Bradke
Year: 2023
Venue: Cell Reports Medicine
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.100948
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9975320

Abstract:
Roussel et al. 1 provide new insight into mecencephalic locomotor region (MLR) stimulation to treat spinal cord injury in mice. Previously, it was unclear which part of the MLR to target.
