Title: Natural eutectogels: sustainable catalytic systems for C–C bond formation reactions
Authors: Beatriz Saavedra, Alessandro Meli, Carla Rizzo, Diego J. Ramón, Francesca D’Anna
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc01647d
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3192131732

Abstract:
Natural eutectogels are used as organized reaction media to perform C–C bond formation reactions in a sustainable way.
