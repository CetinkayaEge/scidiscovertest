Title: Innovation capital, sustainable entrepreneurial orientation, and the moderating role of entrepreneurial resilience
Authors: Wael Hatem Nasser
Year: 2021
Venue: Entrepreneurial Business and Economics Review
DOI: https://doi.org/10.15678/eber.2021.090305
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3177064953

Abstract:
The objective of the article is to know the nature of the relationship between creative capital as a positive personal touch capable of promoting sustainable entrepreneurial orientation (SEO). To increase the effectiveness of this orientation, I identified entrepreneurial resilience as an interactive variable.
