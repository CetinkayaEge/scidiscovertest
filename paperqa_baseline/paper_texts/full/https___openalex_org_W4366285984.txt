Title: The Global Conversation on Sustainability
Authors: Juliana L. Vidal, João Borges
Year: 2023
Venue: Chemistry International
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1515/ci-2023-0204
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4366285984

Abstract:
Abstract Since 25 September 2022, the International Younger Chemists Network (IYCN) and the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC) are jointly organizing the Global Conversation on Sustainability (GCS, https://www.gcs-day.org ) in an annual fashion. This day has been chosen because it marks the anniversary of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs) [1].
