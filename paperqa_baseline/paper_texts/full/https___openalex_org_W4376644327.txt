Title: Education and Sustainable Development
Authors: Anagrolia Faustino, Inderpreet Kaur
Year: 2023
Venue: Asian Journal of Advanced Research and Reports
DOI: https://doi.org/10.9734/ajarr/2023/v17i7497
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4376644327

Abstract:
Education has been known as the best tool in the world for developing a human being from darkness to lightness. Different research scholars argue that education can be formal, informal, or non-formal. Apart from that, a better and more tasteful education should bring about changes socially, politically, economically, and culturally. Human beings manage to handle and solve different problems through education. This will also solve the misconception that comes from the other side of the illiterate population about the concept of being educated. This will empower a person with the knowledge to face and develop the environment. Having these skills is what is called education and sustainable development. The study discusses ideas about the goals of education and sustainable development. Learners live on what they have learned from school. The study insists on more consideration being given to practical issues than theory. The study explains sustainable development from three perspectives: economy, society, and environment. Skills make the world satisfied. Education and Sustainable Development (ESD) and satisfying the environment.
