Title: Organizational Legitimacy: Earning &amp; Sustaining Public Trust
Authors: Neya Global, Anna Neya Kazanskaia
Year: 2025
Venue: 
DOI: https://doi.org/10.64357/legitimacy-2025
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4413478837

Abstract:
This book examines organizational legitimacy as a foundation for sustainability and influence in the non-profit sector. It explores the multidimensional nature of legitimacy—including pragmatic, moral, cognitive, and representational aspects—and demonstrates how non-profits can strategically cultivate and sustain public trust. Combining theoretical perspectives with applied frameworks, it offers tools for assessing legitimacy, navigating threats, and strengthening accountability in complex environments.
