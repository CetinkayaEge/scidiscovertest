Title: New Trends in Sustainable Business and Consumption
Authors: Tăchiciu, Laurențiu
Year: 2022
Venue: Proceeding Papers
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24818/basiq/2022/
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W2285763200

Abstract:
Globalization and technological advances are shaping a new economy, accelerating societal changes and raising sustainability concerns. The twenty first century has begun under the aegis of transformations and tensions which affect business and consumption patterns alike. These transformations come with new opportunities, but also with new threats, challenging the society capacity to foresee mutations, to make responsible choices for the future, to creatively conciliate aspirations with possibilities and resources. All these call for scholars’ contribution to identifying new trends, assessing implications, providing new paradigms and practical solutions, informing policy makers and business leaders, as well as to accelerating new knowledge dissemination.
