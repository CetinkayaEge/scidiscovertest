Title: A sustainable waste-to-protein system to maximise waste resource utilisation for developing food- and feed-grade protein solutions
Authors: Ellen Piercy, Willy Verstraete, Peter R. Ellis, Mason Banks, Johan Rockström, Pete Smith, Oliver C. Witard, Jason P. Hallett, Christer Högstrand, Geoffrey Knott, Ai Karwati, Henintso Felamboahangy Rasoarahona, Andrew G. W. Leslie, Yiying He, Miao Guo
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2gc03095k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4313382249

Abstract:
Analysis of an integrated waste-to-protein system for a sustainable protein future to ameliorate the rising global hunger pandemic.
