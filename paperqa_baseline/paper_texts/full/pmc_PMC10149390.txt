Title: Full calcium jacket: Conservative management of a traumatic aortic injury
Authors: Rishi Tripathi, Hesham Ellauzi, Mohammad A. Zafar, Bulat A. Ziganshin, John A. Elefteriades
Year: 2023
Venue: Journal of Vascular Surgery Cases, Innovations and Techniques
DOI: 10.1016/j.jvscit.2023.101132
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10149390

Abstract:
We describe the case of an elderly male patient who presented with a proximal descending aortic aneurysm after a motorcycle accident in 1977. We concluded that the aorta had been transected at that time. In a rather unconventional manner, the aneurysm developed a circumferential layer of calcification that provided mechanical stability and likely prevented further degeneration. We chose not to pursue surgical intervention at the late stage of his presentation. The patient has been followed up for a period of 30 years, with no change in the size and shape of the now completely calcified aneurysm. Graphical abstract
