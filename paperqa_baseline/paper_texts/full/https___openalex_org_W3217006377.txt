Title: Electrocatalysis enabled transformation of earth-abundant water, nitrogen and carbon dioxide for a sustainable future
Authors: Kaili Liu, Pengfei Cao, Wei Chen, Collins I. Ezeh, Zijian Chen, Yonglan Luo, Qian Liu, Haitao Zhao, Zhenhua Rui, Shuyan Gao, Zongyou Yin, Xuping Sun, Xue‐Feng Yu
Year: 2021
Venue: Materials Advances
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1ma00814e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3217006377

Abstract:
This review summarizes the recent advances in the development of electrocatalysts to facilitate the conversion of H 2 O, N 2 and CO 2 into essential global products, providing an in-depth understanding of the design of high-performance electrocatalysts.
