Title: Zoonotic Transmission of mcr-1 Colistin Resistance Gene from Small-Scale Poultry Farms, Vietnam
Authors: Nguyen Vinh Trung, Sébastien Matamoros, Juan J. Carrique-Mas, Nguyen Huu Nghia, Nguyen Thi Nhung, Tran Thi Bich Chieu, Ho Huynh Mai, Willemien van Rooijen, James Campbell, Jaap A. Wagenaar, Anita Hardon, Nguyen Thi Nhu Mai, Thai Quoc Hieu, Guy Thwaites, Menno D. de Jong, Constance Schultsz, Ngo Thi Hoa
Year: 2017
Venue: Emerging Infectious Diseases
DOI: 10.3201/eid2303.161553
Paper ID: pmc_PMC5382726

Abstract:
We investigated the consequences of colistin use in backyard chicken farms in Vietnam by examining the prevalence of mcr-1 in fecal samples from chickens and humans. Detection of mcr-1 –carrying bacteria in chicken samples was associated with colistin use and detection in human samples with exposure to mcr-1 –positive chickens.
