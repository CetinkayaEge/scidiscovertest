Title: Prevention of Thelazia callipaeda Reinfection among Humans
Authors: Marija Trenkić, Suzana Tasić-Otašević, Marcos Antonio Bezerra-Santos, Marko Stalević, Aleksandar Petrović, Domenico Otranto
Year: 2023
Venue: Emerging Infectious Diseases
DOI: 10.3201/eid2904.221610
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10045684

Abstract:
Thelazia callipaeda is a zoonotic vector-borne nematode that infects and causes eye disease among a wide range of domestic and wild mammals, including humans. We describe an unusual case of reinfection by this nematode in Serbia and call for a focus on preventive measures in endemic areas.
