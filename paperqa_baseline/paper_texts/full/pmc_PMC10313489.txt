Title: Takotsubo Cardiomyopathy Triggered by Emotional Stress From the Russia-Ukraine War
Authors: Sarah Rikabi, Sachin Kumar, Gautam Shah
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101895
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313489

Abstract:
Stress-induced cardiomyopathy presents like acute coronary syndrome and is triggered by emotional stress or critical illness. Increased incidence has been reported during the COVID-19 pandemic and natural disasters. We describe a case of stress-induced cardiomyopathy as an indirect consequence of the Russia-Ukraine war.
