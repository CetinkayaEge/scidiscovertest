Title: Catalysis at the intersection of sustainable chemistry and a circular economy
Authors: Ali Abbas, Megan Cross, Xiaoguang Duan, Steffen Jeschke, Muxina Konarova, George W. Huber, Adam F. Lee, Emma C. Lovell, Jason Y. C. Lim, Anastasios Polyzos, Ryan M. Richards, Karen Wilson
Year: 2024
Venue: One Earth
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oneear.2024.04.018
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4397002662

Abstract:
The modern (petro)chemical industry has supported substantial development but uses unsustainable fossil resources detrimental to the Earth's ecosystem via climate change and chemical pollution. This commentary highlights how catalysts can support development of critical fuel and chemicals manufacturing using sustainable waste resources to transition the industry from a linear to a circular economy.
