Title: What Influences the Sustainable Food Consumption Behaviours of University Students? A Systematic Review
Authors: Lucía Aguirre Sánchez, Zayne Milena Roa‐Díaz, Magda Gamba, Giorgia Grisotto, Ana Maria Moreno Londoño, Blanca Patricia Mantilla-Uribe, Alba Yaneth Rincón Méndez, Mónica Ballesteros, Doris Kopp‐Heim, Beatrice Minder, L. Suzanne Suggs, Oscar H. Franco
Year: 2021
Venue: International Journal of Public Health
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/ijph.2021.1604149
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3197923602

Abstract:
Healthy lifestyle of sustainable food consumers suggests possible synergies between human health and sustainability in terms of motivations for food choice. Moderation effects of social and situational factors on personal factors reveal opportunities to design and examine the effects of choice architecture interventions.
