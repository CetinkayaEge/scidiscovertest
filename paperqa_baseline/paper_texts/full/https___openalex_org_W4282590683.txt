Title: Residents’ satisfaction with sustainable tourism: the moderating role of environmental awareness
Authors: Ikram Ullah Khan, Safeer Ullah Khan, Salman Khan
Year: 2022
Venue: Tourism Critiques Practice and Theory
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/trc-04-2022-0007
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4282590683

Abstract:
Purpose The purpose of this paper is to empirically evaluate the significant influence of four sustainability dimensions in predicting the residents’ satisfaction with the development of sustainable tourism in the emerging tourism industry of Pakistan.
