Title: Considering sustainability thresholds for BECCS in IPCC and biodiversity assessments
Authors: Felix Creutzig, Karl‐Heinz Erb, Helmut Haberl, Christian Hof, Carol Hunsberger, Stephanie Roe
Year: 2021
Venue: GCB Bioenergy
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/gcbb.12798
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3133187083

Abstract:
The majority of scenarios that meet the goals of the Paris agreements exceed sustainability and precautionary thresholds in land, biodiversity and BECCS potentials. Risks may be best avoided by demand-side driven rapid decarbonization and less land-intensive carbon dioxide removal technologies.
