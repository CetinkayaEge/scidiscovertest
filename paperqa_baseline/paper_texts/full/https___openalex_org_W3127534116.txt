Title: Are lignin-derived monomers and polymers truly sustainable? An in-depth green metrics calculations approach
Authors: Sami Fadlallah, Pallabi Sinha Roy, Gil Garnier, Kei Saito, Florent Allais
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d0gc03982a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3127534116

Abstract:
The green aspects of the lignin-derived monomers and polymers have been analysed. A different viewpoint has been provided to encourage researchers to use simple and yet effective green metrics calculations in the development of sustainable syntheses.
