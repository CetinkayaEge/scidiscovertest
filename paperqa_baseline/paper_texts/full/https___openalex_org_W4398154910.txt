Title: Asymmetric nexus between green technologies, economic policy uncertainty, and environmental sustainability: Evidence from Algeria
Authors: Brahim Bergougui, Samer Mehibel, Reda Hamza Boudjana
Year: 2024
Venue: Journal of Environmental Management
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2024.121172
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4398154910

Abstract:
• Investigates the asymmetric effect of several factors on carbon emissions. • This study focuses on Algeria for the period from Q1:1985 to Q4:2022.. • Positive shocks in green technologies and economic policy uncertainty reduce emissions. • Negative shocks in green technologies and economic policy uncertainty are not sufficient to reduce emissions. • The EKC hypothesis is valid.
