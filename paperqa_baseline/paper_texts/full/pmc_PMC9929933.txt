Title: A loosely coordinated interaction site for arachidonic acid on ASICs
Authors: Robert C. Klipp, John R. Bankston
Year: 2023
Venue: The Journal of General Physiology
DOI: 10.1085/jgp.202213307
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9929933

Abstract:
Molecular dynamics simulations reveal a putative interaction surface for PUFAs on TM1 of ASICs that is not tightly conserved between isoforms.
