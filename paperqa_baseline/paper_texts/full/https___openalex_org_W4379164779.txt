Title: Cathode regeneration and upcycling of spent LIBs: toward sustainability
Authors: Xiang Xiao, Li Wang, Yingqiang Wu, Youzhi Song, Zonghai Chen, Xiangming He
Year: 2023
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3ee00746d
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4379164779

Abstract:
Cathode regeneration and upcycling technologies, aimed at non-destructively recovering and upgrading the electrochemical performance of degraded materials, show excellent flexibility and potential for transforming the LIB industry from a resource-based to a circular economy.
