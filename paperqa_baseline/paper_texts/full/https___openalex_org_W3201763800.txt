Title: Sustainable by design, large Stokes shift benzothiadiazole derivatives for efficient luminescent solar concentrators
Authors: Chiara Ceriani, Francesca Corsini, Giuseppe Mattioli, Sara Mattiello, D. Testa, Riccardo Pó, Chiara Botta, Gianmarco Griffini, Luca Beverina
Year: 2021
Venue: Journal of Materials Chemistry C
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1tc03536c
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3201763800

Abstract:
The combination of a de novo design approach and micellar catalysis enables the preparation of innovative luminophores connecting efficiency and sustainability for the preparation of thin film luminescent solar concentrators.
