Title: Microwave-assisted synthesis of nanomaterials: a green chemistry perspective and sustainability assessment
Authors: T. Sajini, Jebin Joseph
Year: 2025
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d5su00584a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4413959458

Abstract:
This review highlights sustainable microwave-assisted synthesis of nanomaterials, focusing on green chemistry, environmental benefits, and comparative insights for scalable, eco-friendly production aligned with sustainability goals.
