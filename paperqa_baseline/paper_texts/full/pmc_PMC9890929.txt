Title: Visible-light-induced selective defluoroalkylations of polyfluoroarenes with alcohols †
Authors: Wengang Xu, Qi Shao, Congjian Xia, Qiao Zhang, Yadi Xu, Yingguo Liu, Mingbo Wu
Year: 2022
Venue: Chemical Science
DOI: 10.1039/d2sc06290a
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9890929

Abstract:
To provide α-polyfluoroarylalcohols, a novel protocol for the selective defluoroalkylation of polyfluoroarenes with easily accessible alcohols was reported via the cooperation of photoredox and hydrogen atom transfer (HAT) strategies with the assistance of Lewis acids under visible light irradiation. The protocol featured broad scope, excellent regioselectivity for both C–H and C–F bond cleavages, and mild conditions. Mechanistic studies suggested that the reaction occurred through Lewis acid-promoted HAT to provide an alkyl radical and sequential addition to polyfluoroarenes. Impressively, the regioselectivity for C–F cleavage was verified with the Fukui function. The feasibility and application of this protocol on fluoroarene synthesis were well illustrated by gram-scale synthesis under both batch and flow conditions, late-stage decoration of bioactive compounds, and further transformations of the fluoroarylalcohols. A green and feasible synthetic protocol for α-polyfluoroarylalcohols was developed with polyfluoroarenes and alcohols via Lewis acid-assisted synergistic photoredox and hydrogen-atom transfer catalysis under batch or flow conditions.
