Title: Nature-Based Solutions for More Sustainable Cities – A Framework Approach for Planning and Evaluation
Authors: Barker, Adam; id_orcid 0000-0001-8835-7837, Felliu, Efren, Garcia-Blanco, Gemma, Kwiacinska, Kornelia, Pedrola, Blanca
Year: 2021
Venue: 
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/9781800436367

Abstract:
Nature-Based Solutions for More Sustainable Cities makes a clear case of performances, impacts, and benefits generated by NBS in cities providing a comprehensive framework approach to understand the real and full potential of NBS at the urban level.
