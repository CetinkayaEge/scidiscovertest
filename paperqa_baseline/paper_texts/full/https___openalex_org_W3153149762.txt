Title: The impact of sustainability practices on financial performance: empirical evidence from Sweden
Authors: Đức Cường Phạm, Thị Ngọc Ánh, Thanh Nga Doan, Thi Xuan Hong Nguyen, Thi Kim Yen Pham
Year: 2021
Venue: Cogent Business & Management
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/23311975.2021.1912526
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3153149762

Abstract:
This study aims at empirically exploring the influence of sustainability practices on the finan cial performance of 116 listed Swedish companies in the year 2019. The research findings indicate a positive relationship between corporate sustainability and financial performance that is measured by earnings yield, return on asset, return on equity and return on capital employed. However, when it comes to a market-based financial measure, Tobin's Q, the result is inconclusive.
