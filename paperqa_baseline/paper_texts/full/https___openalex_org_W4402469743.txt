Title: ESG investment performance and global attention to sustainability
Authors: Thanh Nam Vu, Heikki Lehkonen, Juha-Pekka Junttila, Brian M. Lucey
Year: 2024
Venue: The North American Journal of Economics and Finance
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.najef.2024.102287
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4402469743

Abstract:
• The relationship between ESG and returns is weak, suggesting ESG information is well reflected in stock prices. • In the early 2010s, pursuing sustainable investments may have involved some compromise in financial performance. • ESG investment performance is influenced by global attention to sustainability. • Companies with strong management gain advantages during periods of increasing global sustainability attention. We analyze ESG-based investments in stocks across 23 developed markets using daily data from 2004 to 2022. The findings suggest a weak relationship between the ESG ratings and expected returns, with some evidence of modest underperformance of high ESG stocks compared to lower-rated ones in specific periods. This outcome indicates that stock prices have already reflected ESG information, and well-known asset pricing factors can effectively capture the returns of portfolios based on ESG ratings. However, the strength of this relationship depends on global attention to sustainability, where high ESG-rated stocks tend to gain advantages during unexpected attention increases, highlighting the dynamic, nonlinear nature of this relationship.
