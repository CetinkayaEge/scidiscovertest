Title: Sparsentan: First Approval
Authors: Yahiya Y. Syed
Year: 2023
Venue: Drugs
DOI: 10.1007/s40265-023-01864-x
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10232600

Abstract:
Sparsentan (FILSPARI™) is an oral, dual endothelin angiotensin receptor antagonist that is being developed by Travere Therapeutics for the treatment of immunoglobulin A (IgA) nephropathy and focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS). In February 2023, sparsentan received accelerated approval in the USA for reducing proteinuria in adults with primary IgA nephropathy who are at risk of rapid disease progression. This article summarizes the milestones in the development of sparsentan leading to this first approval for IgA nephropathy.
