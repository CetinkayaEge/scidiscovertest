Manager, Ash Coordination &amp; Materials, Haldia Energy Limited Baneswarchak, Haldia, 721658 Fly ash is a fine powder generated during the burning of pulverized coal in thermal power plants. This industrial waste may create significant environmental problems when released into the atmosphere. In the past, vast majority of the material generated each year was held in ash dams or similar dumps but in recent years a number of technologies have been developed for the beneficial utilization of fly ash in different fields. Hence, to mitigate these impacts, Haldia Energy Limited (HEL) has undertaken eco-friendly manners for Fly ash utilization since its inception to reduce the environmental pollution impacts and save the environment.
