Title: Towards developing fully sustainable elastomers: the role of chemistry
Authors: Paulina Wiśniewska, Peyman Ezzati, Józef T. Haponiuk, Aleksander Hejna, X. Colom, Mohammad Reza Saeb
Year: 2025
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4gc03802a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4406227200

Abstract:
This review outlines sustainable elastomers and green ingredients (derived from renewable resources) used in their formulation are summarized, analyzed, and discussed.
