Title: Biogenic colourants in the textile industry – a promising and sustainable alternative to synthetic dyes
Authors: Richard Fried, Ilinca Oprea, Karin Fleck, Florian Rudroff
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc02968a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3217439684

Abstract:
This review compares chemically and microbiologically derived textile dyes in terms of the production process, chemical structures and molecule properties. Main challenges for the application are discussed to highlight solutions for a greener future.
