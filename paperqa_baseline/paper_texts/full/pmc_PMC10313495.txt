Title: Surgical Excision of a Left Atrial Mobile Stalked Calcification Arising After Multiple Radiofrequency Ablations
Authors: Tadanori Nakata, Naoyuki Miwa, Yoshikazu Sato, Kazuya Yamao, Hitoshi Hachiya
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101883
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313495

Abstract:
We report a rare case of a mobile ectopic calcification in the left atrium requiring surgical excision 9 years after multiple atrial fibrillation ablations. ( Level of Difficulty: Intermediate. ) Central Illustration
