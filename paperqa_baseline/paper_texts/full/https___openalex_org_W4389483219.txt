Title: Protic ionic liquids for sustainable uses
Authors: Josh J. Bailey, Emily L. Byrne, Peter Goodrich, Paul Kavanagh, Małgorzata Swadźba‐Kwaśny
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc03297c
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4389483219

Abstract:
This review provides an overview of the current state-of-the-art and major trends in the application of protic ionic liquids (PILs) to sustainable chemistry.
