Title: Harbor Porpoise Deaths Associated with Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae , the Netherlands, 2021
Authors: Lonneke L. IJsseldijk, Lineke Begeman, Birgitta Duim, Andrea Gröne, Marja J.L. Kik, Mirjam D. Klijnstra, Jan Lakemeyer, Mardik F. Leopold, Bas B. Oude Munnink, Mariel ten Doeschate, Linde van Schalkwijk, Aldert Zomer, Linda van der Graaf-van Bloois, Els M. Broens
Year: 2023
Venue: Emerging Infectious Diseases
DOI: 10.3201/eid2904.221698
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10045706

Abstract:
In August 2021, a large-scale mortality event affected harbor porpoises ( Phocoena phocoena ) in the Netherlands. Pathology and ancillary testing of 22 animals indicated that the most likely cause of death was Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae infection. This zoonotic agent poses a health hazard for cetaceans and possibly for persons handling cetacean carcasses.
