Title: Environmentally sustainable, high-performance lignin-derived universal adhesive
Authors: Sandip K. Singh, Kolja Ostendorf, Markus Euring, Kai Zhang
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2gc00014h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4214751660

Abstract:
A lignin-based adhesive was developed with the formation of new C–O, C–N, and C–C bonds that contributed to adhesive performance on versatile substrates.
