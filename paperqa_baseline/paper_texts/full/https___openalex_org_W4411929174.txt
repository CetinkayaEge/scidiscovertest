Title: Spray-Drying Microencapsulation of Natural Bioactives: Advances in Sustainable Wall Materials
Authors: Lauryna Pudžiuvelytė, Eglė Petrauskaitė, Jolita Stabrauskienė, Jurga Bernatonienė
Year: 2025
Venue: Pharmaceuticals
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18070963
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4411929174

Abstract:
: Spray-drying-based microencapsulation is an advanced technology that effectively protects sensitive active ingredients and allows for wider industrial food, pharmaceutical, and cosmetic applications. In the future, more attention is expected to be paid to personalized formulations, stimulated release systems, and sustainable wall materials from by-products.
