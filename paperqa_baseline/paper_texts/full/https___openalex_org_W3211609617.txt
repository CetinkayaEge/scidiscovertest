Title: An ionic hydrogel with stimuli-responsive, self-healable and injectable characteristics for the targeted and sustained delivery of doxorubicin in the treatment of breast cancer
Authors: Muzammil Kuddushi, Deepak K. Pandey, Dheeraj K. Singh, Jitendra Mata, Naved I. Malek
Year: 2021
Venue: Materials Advances
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1ma00835h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3211609617

Abstract:
Targeted and sustained release of doxorubicin was achieved through a self-healable and injectable ionic hydrogel.
