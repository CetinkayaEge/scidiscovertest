Title: The Global Lifeline: A Staple Crop Sustaining Two-Thirds of the World’s Population
Authors: Jabeen Asma, D. Subrahmanyam, D. Krishnaveni
Year: 2023
Venue: Agriculture Archives.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.51470/agri.2023.2.3.15
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4401645430

Abstract:
Rice stands as a global lifeline, serving as the staple food crop for over two-thirds of the world’s population. This article explores the profound significance, challenges, and prospects of rice cultivation in sustaining human nutrition and livelihoods worldwide. Despite its ubiquity, rice production faces multifaceted challenges ranging from climate change impacts to pests and diseases. Innovative solutions, including climate-resilient varieties and sustainable agricultural practices, offer hope for enhancing rice production efficiency and resilience. Effective policies, international cooperation, and investments in research and infrastructure are crucial for ensuring equitable access to rice and promoting sustainable food systems, embracing innovation and fostering collaboration will be essential in building resilient food systems capable of meeting the nutritional needs of a growing population while safeguarding environmental resources. This article underscores the critical role of rice in global food security and emphasizes the importance of collective action in addressing the challenges facing rice production and distribution.
