Title: Solving data quality issues of fundus images in real-world settings by ophthalmic AI
Authors: Zhongwen Li, Wei Chen
Year: 2023
Venue: Cell Reports Medicine
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.100951
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9975325

Abstract:
Liu et al. 1 develop a deep-learning-based flow cytometry-like image quality classifier, DeepFundus, for the automated, high-throughput, and multidimensional classification of fundus image quality. DeepFundus significantly improves the real-world performance of established artificial intelligence diagnostics in detecting multiple retinopathies.
