Title: Comparing pathways for electricity-based production of dimethoxymethane as a sustainable fuel
Authors: Jannik Burre, Dominik Bongartz, Sarah Deutz, Chalachew Mebrahtu, Ole Osterthun, Ruiyan Sun, Simon Völker, André Bardow, Jürgen Klankermayer, Regina Palkovits, Alexander Mitsos
Year: 2021
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1ee00689d
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3175291402

Abstract:
A hierarchical methodology for process design and evaluation reveals how the remarkable achievements in catalyst development for dimethoxymethane (DMM) synthesis can make DMM a sustainable future e-fuel.
