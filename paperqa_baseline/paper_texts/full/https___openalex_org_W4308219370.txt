Title: From rocks to bioactive compounds: a journey through the global P(v) organophosphorus industry and its sustainability
Authors: Sosthène Ung, Chao‐Jun Li
Year: 2022
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2su00015f
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4308219370

Abstract:
This tutorial review showcases the diversity &amp; utility of organophosphorus molecules, focusing on P( v ) structures. Their rich nomenclature, application &amp; industrial synthesis will be highlighted within the context of sustainable chemistry.
