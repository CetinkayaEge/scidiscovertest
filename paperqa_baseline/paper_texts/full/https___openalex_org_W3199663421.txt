Title: Assessing smallholder sustainable intensification in the Ethiopian highlands
Authors: James Hammond, Mark T. van Wijk, Nils Teufel, Kindu Mekonnen, Peter S. Thorne
Year: 2021
Venue: Agricultural Systems
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agsy.2021.103266
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3199663421

Abstract:
Sustainable intensification is one approach to increasing food production without undermining sustainability goals. In recent years new tools and indicators have been developed for broad-based assessment of sustainable intensification. However, most of these tools have been applied at field level and assessing individual technologies, while integrated assessments of multiple novel practices at farm-to-village level are lacking.
