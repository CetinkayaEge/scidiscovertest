Title: A solution to break the salt barrier for high-rate sustainable solar desalination
Authors: Lin Zhu, Lei Sun, Hong Zhang, Hüsnü Aslan, Ye Sun, Yudong Huang, Federico Rosei, Miao Yu
Year: 2021
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1ee00113b
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3138904658

Abstract:
We present a strategy to break the salt barrier for sustainable solar steam generation from seawater, by constructing an efficient water–salt loop and employing a highly porous, super-hydrophobic material with high photothermal conversion capability.
