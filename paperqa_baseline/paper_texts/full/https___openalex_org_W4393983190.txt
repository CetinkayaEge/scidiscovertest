Title: Sustainable biodegradable coatings for food packaging: challenges and opportunities
Authors: Fatemeh Jahangiri, Amar K. Mohanty, Manjusri Misra
Year: 2024
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc02647g
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4393983190

Abstract:
Bio-based and petroleum-based biodegradable coatings: materials, methods, and modifications to improve the performance of polymeric substrates for plastic and paper-based food packaging applications.
