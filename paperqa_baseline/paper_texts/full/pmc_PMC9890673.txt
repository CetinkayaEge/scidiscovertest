Title: Small change for big improvement in the preparation of the key intermediate N 1 , N 3 -disubstituted 1,3,5-triazone of ensitrelvir †
Authors: Wei Hu, Yuanchang Liu, Xiang Zhang, Panpan Zheng, Feifei Yang, Guangyang Guo, Xin Xie, Jiuzhong Huang, Weiming Chen
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Advances
DOI: 10.1039/d2ra07844a
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9890673

Abstract:
In this study, the key intermediate N 1 , N 3 -disubstituted 1,3,5-triazone of ensitrelvir fumaric acid, approved in Japan for the treatment of SARS-CoV-2 infection under the emergency regulatory approval system, was produced from S -ethylisothiourea hydrobromide and aminomethyl triazole with CDI by four-step telescoped strategy including CDI-activated, condensation, CDI-cyclization, and N 1 -alkylation. The strategy with simple conditions and operations had a total yield of 53% on a gram scale. The strategy for synthesizing the key N 1 , N 3 -disubstituted 1,3,5-triazone intermediate of ensitrelvir might provide a new avenue for further research and development of ensitrelvir analogs. A four-step telescoped strategy for synthesis of the key intermediate of ensitrelvir, approved in Japan for the treatment of SARS-CoV-2 infection under the emergency regulatory approval system, was developed.
