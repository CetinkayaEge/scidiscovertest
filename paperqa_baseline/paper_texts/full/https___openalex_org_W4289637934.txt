Title: Reimagining research ethics to include environmental sustainability: a principled approach, including a case study of data-driven health research
Authors: Gabrielle Samuel, Cristina Richie
Year: 2022
Venue: Journal of Medical Ethics
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/jme-2022-108489
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4289637934

We argue that we need to broaden this notion of individual risk to consider issues associated with environmental sustainability. This is because research is associated with carbon emissions and other environmental impacts, both of which cause climate change health hazards. We introduce how bioethics frameworks have considered notions of environmental sustainability and draw on these to help develop a framework suitable for researchers. We provide a case study of data-driven health research to apply our framework.
