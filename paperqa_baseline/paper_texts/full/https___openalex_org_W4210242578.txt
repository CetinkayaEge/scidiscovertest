Title: Application of agro-waste-mediated silica nanoparticles to sustainable agriculture
Authors: Pooja Goswami, Jyoti Mathur
Year: 2022
Venue: Bioresources and Bioprocessing
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s40643-022-00496-5
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4210242578

Abstract:
NPs have a favorable effect on E. sativa growth and germination, enhancing plant production which helps to improve the sustainable agriculture farming and acting as a possible antifungal agent against plant pathogenic fungi.
