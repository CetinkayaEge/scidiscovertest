Title: Relevance of international partnerships in the implementation of the UN Sustainable Development Goals
Authors: Walter Leal Filho, Tony Wall, Jelena Barbir, Gabriela Nagle Alverio, Maria Alzira Pimenta Dinis, Julianna Paola Ramírez Lozano
Year: 2022
Venue: Nature Communications
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28230-x
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4210451464

Abstract:
To achieve Sustainable Development Goal SDG 17, which focuses on international cooperation, partnerships, will be vital. In this comment, we examine the key obstacles such as vested economic interests that will need to be overcome for the successful implementation of SDG 17. Sustainable Development Goal 17 focuses on partnerships that can enable the achievement of other SDGs. In this comment the authors examine the obstacles to the success of SDG 17 and suggest measures to overcome these.
