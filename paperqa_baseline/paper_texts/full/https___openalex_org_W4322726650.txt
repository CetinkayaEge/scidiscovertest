Title: Vaccines for a sustainable planet
Authors: Simone Pecetta, Arindam Nandi, Charlie Weller, Vanessa Harris, Helen A. Fletcher, Francesco Berlanda Scorza, Mariagrazia Pizza, David Salisbury, Richard Moxon, Steve Black, David E. Bloom, Rino Rappuoli
Year: 2023
Venue: Science Translational Medicine
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.adf1093
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4322726650

Abstract:
The health of the planet is one objective of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals. Vaccines can affect not only human health but also planet health by reducing poverty, preserving microbial diversity, reducing antimicrobial resistance, and preventing an increase in pandemics that is fueled partly by climate change.
