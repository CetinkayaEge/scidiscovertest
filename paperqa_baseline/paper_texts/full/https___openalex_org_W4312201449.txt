Title: The Impact of Sustainability Disclosures on Value of Companies Following Digital Transformation Strategies
Authors: Sergei Grishunin, Eugenia Naumova, Ekaterina Burova, Svetlana Suloeva, Tatyana Nekrasova
Year: 2022
Venue: International Journal of Technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.14716/ijtech.v13i7.6194
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4312201449

Abstract:
Sustainable growth is the key global priority, and environmental, social and governance (ESG) objectives have become the main point of attention in companies’ digital transformation strategies. ESG and digital transformation reinforce each other
