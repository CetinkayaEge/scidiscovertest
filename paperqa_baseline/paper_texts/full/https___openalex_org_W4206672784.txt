Title: Sustainability-inspired upcycling of waste polyethylene terephthalate plastic into porous carbon for CO2 capture
Authors: Xiangzhou Yuan, Nallapaneni Manoj Kumar, Boris Brigljevic﻿́, Shuangjun Li, Shuai Deng, Manhee Byun, Boreum Lee, Carol Sze Ki Lin, Daniel C.W. Tsang, Ki Bong Lee, Shauhrat S. Chopra, Hankwon Lim, Yong Sik Ok
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc03600a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4206672784

Abstract:
Industrial-scale upcycling of waste polyethylene terephthalate (PET) plastic into porous carbon globally for CO 2 capture was verified as a multifunctional alternative to conventional CO 2 absorption and plastic waste management technologies.
