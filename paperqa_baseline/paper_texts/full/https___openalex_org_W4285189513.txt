Title: Sustainable water generation: grand challenges in continuous atmospheric water harvesting
Authors: Primož Poredoš, Shan He, Chenxi Wang, Fangfang Deng, R.Z. Wang
Year: 2022
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2ee01234k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4285189513

Abstract:
This perspective provides possible development paths for future advancement of continuously operated sorption and radiative sky cooling atmospheric water harvesting on material and system levels.
