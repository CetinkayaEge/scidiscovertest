Title: T cell biomarkers come to the fore in cancer immunotherapy
Authors: Lozan Sheriff, Alastair Copland, David Bending
Year: 2023
Venue: Cell Reports Medicine
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.100989
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10140386

Abstract:
A comprehensive study by van der Sluis et al. 1 demonstrates immunotherapeutic targeting of OX40 and PD-L1 results in enhanced tumor clearance, which is linked to the dynamic emergence of distinct subsets of CD8 + T cells.
