Title: Chemical recycling of polyester textile wastes: shifting towards sustainability
Authors: Théo El Darai, Alexandra Ter-Halle, Muriel Blanzat, Guillaume Despras, Valérie Sartor, Guillaume Bordeau, Armand Lattes, Sophie Franceschi, Stéphanie Cassel, Nadia Chouini-Lalanne, Emile Pérez, Christophe Déjugnat, Jean‐Christophe Garrigues
Year: 2024
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4gc00911h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4398216423

Abstract:
Amongst all synthetic polymers used in the clothing industry, polyethylene terephthalate (PET) is the most widely used polyester, its fibres representing half the total PET global market (in comparison bottle PET being less than a third).
