Title: Circular battery design: investing in sustainability and profitability
Authors: Andreas Wolf, Felix Nagler, Philip Daubinger, Christoph Neef, Karl Mandel, Andreas Flegler, Guinevere A. Giffin
Year: 2024
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ee03418j
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4403394518

Abstract:
The market share of low-cost battery chemistries, which offer little to no recycling profitability with current methods, is growing. Design for circularity could be the key to reducing costs and enhancing sustainability for these batteries.
