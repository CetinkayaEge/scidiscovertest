Title: Fluorine-free ionic liquid electrolytes for sustainable neodymium recovery using an electrochemical approach
Authors: Kalani Periyapperuma, Jennifer M. Pringle, Laura Sanchez-Cupido, Maria Forsyth, Cristina Pozo‐Gonzalo
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc00361e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3158033853

Abstract:
The application of a fluorine-free ionic liquid electrolyte has enabled cleaner and sustainable Nd recovery via electrodeposition.
