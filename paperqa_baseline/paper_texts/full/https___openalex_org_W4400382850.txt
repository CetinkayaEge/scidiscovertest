Title: Electrospinning of sustainable polymers from biomass for active food packaging
Authors: Fuat Topuz, Tamer Uyar
Year: 2024
Venue: Sustainable Food Technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4fb00147h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4400382850

Abstract:
This review outlines the latest developments in using electrospun materials derived from sustainable, biomass-sourced polymers for active food packaging applications.
