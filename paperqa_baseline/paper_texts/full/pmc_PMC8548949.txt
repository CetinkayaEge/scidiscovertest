Title: Association of COVID-19 With New Waiting List Registrations and Liver Transplantation for Alcoholic Hepatitis in the United States
Authors: Maia S. Anderson, Valeria S. M. Valbuena, Craig S. Brown, Seth A. Waits, Christopher J. Sonnenday, Michael Englesbe, Jessica L. Mellinger
Year: 2021
Venue: JAMA Network Open
DOI: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.31132
Paper ID: pmc_PMC8548949

Abstract:
This cross-sectional study examines trends in new waiting list registrations and liver transplantation for alcoholic hepatitis before and during the COVID-19 pandemic in the US.
