Title: The FAIR principles as a key enabler to operationalize safe and sustainable by design approaches
Authors: Achilleas Karakoltzidis, Chiara Laura Battistelli, Cecilia Bossa, Evert A. Bouman, Irantzu Garmendia Aguirre, Ivo Iavicoli, Maryam Zare Jeddi, Spyros Karakitsios, Veruscka Leso, Magnus Løfstedt, Barbara Magagna, Denis Sarigiannis, Erik Schultes, Lya G. Soeteman‐Hernández, Vrishali Subramanian, Penny Nymark
Year: 2024
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4su00171k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4402769303

Abstract:
Safe and sustainable chemicals/materials are critical for achieving European green goals. The novel SSbD framework aims to harmonize assessments during innovation. Here, we discuss the essential role of FAIR data and tools in operationalizing SSbD.
