Title: Impact of ready-made garments (RMG) industries and sustainability: Perspective of the pandemic period in developing country
Authors: Tarekul Islam, Md. Abdul Halim
Year: 2022
Venue: Cleaner Engineering and Technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clet.2022.100567
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4303444719

Abstract:
The goal of this paper is to find out the impact of COVID-19 on the RMG sectors in the developing economy. This study consists of theoretical circumstances and was conducted with the help of several interviews, secondary sources and documents. The findings of this study show that COVID-19 has impacted negatively on the RMG industry, which has delayed the economy of Bangladesh. It shows the destructive influence of COVID-19 on RMG's sector and also shows some steps to control the number of COVID-19 infestations in the RMG's sector of Bangladesh. Additionally, it describes how we can sustain our RMG's sectors during COVID-19 and after it, and also how this study will be helpful for other countries around the world. Finally, it provides a policy implication and suggestions on how to keep sustainability after COVID-19 in this sector.
