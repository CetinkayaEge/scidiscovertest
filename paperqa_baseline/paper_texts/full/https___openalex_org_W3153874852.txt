Title: A critical review on the progress and challenges to a more sustainable, cost competitive synthesis of adipic acid
Authors: Jeovanna Rios, Juliana Lebeau, Tian Yang, Shuai Li, Michael Lynch
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc00638j
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3153874852

Abstract:
A review of recent developments and remaining challenges to the development of greener, cost competitive processes for adipic acid manufacture. Chemical and biotechnological processes from both petroleum based and renewable feedstocks are reviewed.
