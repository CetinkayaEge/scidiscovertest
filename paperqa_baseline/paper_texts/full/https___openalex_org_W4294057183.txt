Title: Mapping the diffusion of circular economy good practices: Success factors and sustainable challenges
Authors: Laura Rocca, Monica Veneziani, Cristian Carini
Year: 2022
Venue: Business Strategy and the Environment
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bse.3235
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4294057183

Abstract:
Abstract Shifting from a linear to a circular economy (CE) is a path promoted from different national and supranational organizations from many years but how that circularity is put in place by businesses from an empirical perspective is still a research gap in the literature. There is the need to stress the study of empirical cases to better understand what are the success factors, the sustainable challenges and the way forward to achieve the goal of a CE. This paper aims to investigate how the circular economy is spreading at the empirical level and with what evolution and opportunities. The framework of the diffusion theory is used to analyse how the CE, as a business model innovation, is widespread. The Italian scenario is taken as background, and 62 good practices of CE are analysed. The results promote a discussion on the attributes of CE, policy implications and future studies to booster the circular changing in society.
