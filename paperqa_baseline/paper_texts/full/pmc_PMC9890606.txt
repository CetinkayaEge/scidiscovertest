Title: Detection of alkali and alkaline earth metal ions using birefringence of hyperswollen lamellar phase †
Authors: Koki Sasaki, Shotaro Matoba, Yoshiaki Uchida, Norikazu Nishiyama
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Advances
DOI: 10.1039/d3ra00135k
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9890606

Abstract:
Chemical sensors have targeted various substances. Most sensors electrically amplify signals. Here, we propose a visual detection system that uses a hyperswollen lamellar phase and detects targets in a solution without electric amplification. Amphiphiles with an oligo(ethylene glycol) chain can catch alkali and alkaline earth metal ions and amplify to macroscopic birefringence.
