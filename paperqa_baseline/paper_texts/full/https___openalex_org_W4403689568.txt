Title: Perspectives for sustainability analysis of scalable perovskite photovoltaics
Authors: Xueyu Tian, Samuel D. Stranks, Jinsong Huang, Vasilis Fthenakis, Yang Yang, Fengqi You
Year: 2024
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ee03956d
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4403689568

Abstract:
We propose a multi-scale analytics and modeling framework to fill the gap in integrating circular solar economy principles with ecosystem and climate commitments, enabling a holistic sustainability analysis of perovskite PVs.
