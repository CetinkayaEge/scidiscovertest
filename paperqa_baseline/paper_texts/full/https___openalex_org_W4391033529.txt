Title: Recent climate-smart innovations in agrifood to enhance producer incomes through sustainable solutions
Authors: Tétédé Rodrigue Christian Konfo, Ayédêguê Biaou Philippe Chabi, Abraham Amoussouga Gero, Camel Lagnika, Félicien Avlessi, Gauthier Biaou, Codjo Koko Dominique Sohounhloue
Year: 2024
Venue: Journal of Agriculture and Food Research
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jafr.2024.100985
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4391033529

Abstract:
Climate change poses significant challenges to the agrifood sector, affecting producers' incomes and food security. This review explores recent climate-smart innovations in agrifood that enhance producers' incomes while promoting sustainable solutions. The importance of addressing climate change in the agrifood sector and its impact on producers' incomes are introduced. Climate-Smart Agriculture and its core principles are defined. The significance of adopting climate-smart practices for the agrifood industry is discussed, emphasizing the potential benefits in terms of productivity, resilience, and income generation for producers. Key climate-smart innovations, including precision agriculture techniques, sustainable farming practices, and the role of digital platforms and data analytics, are highlighted. Real-world examples of agrifood businesses and organizations implementing climate-smart innovations are presented, outlining the benefits and limitations of their approaches. The challenges faced in implementing climate-smart innovations, such as financial barriers, technological limitations, and knowledge gaps, are discussed. Future directions for climate-smart innovations are presented, including the adoption of digital technologies, backchain robotics, 3D printing, and virtual/augmented reality. Overcoming challenges related to cost, accessibility, technical expertise, and resistance to change is crucial for successful implementation. In conclusion, embracing climate-smart innovations in the agrifood sector is essential for enhancing producers’ incomes, promoting sustainability, and ensuring food security despite climate change and evolving market demands.
