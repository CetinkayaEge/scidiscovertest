Title: Managing intermittency of renewable power in sustainable production of methanol, coupled with direct air capture
Authors: George J. Fulham, Paula V. Mendoza-Moreno, Ewa Marek
Year: 2024
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4ee00933a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4399350209

Abstract:
This study leverages worldwide wind data, process modelling, and life cycle assessment to reveal the potential of dynamic methanol production for atmospheric CO 2 drawdown, while handling power intermittency and minimising reliance on reserve storage.
