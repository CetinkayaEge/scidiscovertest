Title: Assessment of Disability and Depression Following Amputation Among Adults in Korea
Authors: Wonyoung Jung, Miso Kim, Hong Jin Jeon, Won Hyuk Chang, Jung Eun Yoo, Kyungdo Han, Dong Wook Shin
Year: 2023
Venue: JAMA Network Open
DOI: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.20873
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10311386

Abstract:
This nationwide, population-based, retrospective cohort study assesses the risk of depression following amputation among adults in Korea.
