Title: A sustainable solvent based on lactic acid and l-cysteine for the regeneration of keratin from waste wool
Authors: Amin Shavandi, Hafez Jafari, Erika Zago, Parinaz Hobbi, Lei Nie, Nelson De Laet
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d0gc04314a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3121993717

Abstract:
Keratin dissolution is the first step toward reusing protein-rich biomass such as waste wool, hair, and feather.
