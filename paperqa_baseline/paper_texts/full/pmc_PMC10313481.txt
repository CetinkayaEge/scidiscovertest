Title: 3D Printed Model-Guided Neonatal Transcatheter Closure of Left Main Coronary Artery-to-Right Ventricle Fistula
Authors: Silvia Scalera, Alberto Clemente, Alessandra Pizzuto, Emanuele Gasparotti, Benigno Marco Fanni, Emanuele Vignali, Katia Capellini, Simona Celi, Giuseppe Santoro
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101869
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313481

Abstract:
We report on a 2-week-old infant with huge left main coronary artery-to-right ventricular outflow tract fistula causing myocardial ischemia due to global coronary steal who was successfully submitted to percutaneous closure guided by a 3-dimensional–printed model using a duct-occluder vascular plug.
