Title: Volunteering, Health, and Well-being of Children and Adolescents in the United States
Authors: Kevin Lanza, Ethan T. Hunt, Dale S. Mantey, Onyinye Omega-Njemnobi, Benjamin Cristol, Steven H. Kelder
Year: 2023
Venue: JAMA Network Open
DOI: 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.15980
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10230312

Abstract:
This cross-sectional study examines whether there is an association between volunteering and well-being among children and adolescents across the United States.
