Title: Reorient the Business Case for Corporate Sustainability
Authors: Michael L. Barnett, Benjamin Cashore, Irene Henriques, Bryan W. Husted, Rajat Panwar, Jonatan Pinkse
Year: 2021
Venue: National University of Singapore
DOI: https://doi.org/10.48558/fn21-my74
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3191147186

Abstract:
Corporate sustainability programs have grown dramatically, but biases have crippled their effectiveness. We identify three critical steps for reform.
