Title: Further promotion of sustainable development goals using science, technology, and innovation
Authors: Huadong Guo, Lei Huang, Dong Liang
Year: 2022
Venue: The Innovation
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xinn.2022.100325
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4295962185

Abstract:
In 2015, 193 United Nations (UN) member states unanimously adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. The 17 sustainable development goals (SDGs) drafted consist of 169 targets with over 230 indicators to be achieved by the year 2030.
