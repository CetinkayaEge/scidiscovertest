Title: The State of Sustainable Markets 2017 - Statistics and Emerging Trends
Authors: Julia Lernoud, Jason Potts, Gregory Sampson, Garibay, S., Lynch, M., Voora, V., Willer, H., Wozniak, J.
Year: 2022
Venue: Organic Eprints (International Centre for Research in Organic Food Systems, and Research Institute of Organic Agriculture)
DOI: https://doi.org/10.34800/fsc-international401
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W2300808005

Abstract:
This report is a product of a partnership funded by the Swiss State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) between the Research Institute of Organic Agriculture (FiBL), the International Institute of Sustainable Development (IISD) and the International Trade Centre (ITC). It offers a pathway for formalizing the reporting process with a view to making data on sustainable markets more accessible to all; and provides a market data survey on Voluntary Sustainability Standards (VSS). Section one gives an overview of the VSS surveyed with a short description and key data; section two includes the production related data for key global sustainability standards across nine commodity sectors, bananas, cocoa, coffee, cotton, forestry, palm oil, soybeans, cane sugar and tea.
