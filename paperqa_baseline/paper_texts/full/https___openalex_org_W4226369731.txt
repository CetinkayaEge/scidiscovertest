Title: Sustainable lithium-ion batteries based on metal-free tannery waste biochar
Authors: Pejman Salimi, Sebastiano Tieuli, Somayeh Taghavi, Eleonora Venezia, Silvio Fugattini, Simone Lauciello, Mirko Prato, Sergio Marras, Tao Li, Michela Signoretto, Paola Costamagna, Remo Proietti Zaccaria
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc04772h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4226369731

Abstract:
In the present study we tackle the sustainable batteries topic by proposing a tannery-based high performance and eco-friendly battery anode.
