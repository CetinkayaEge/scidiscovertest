Title: On digitalization and sustainability transitions
Authors: Allan Dahl Andersen, Koen Frenken, Victor Galaz, Florian Kern, Laurens Klerkx, Matthijs Mouthaan, Laura Piscicelli, Juliet B. Schor, Taneli Vaskelainen
Year: 2021
Venue: Environmental Innovation and Societal Transitions
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eist.2021.09.013
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3205180022

Abstract:
• Transition scholars devote surprisingly little attention to the sustainability of digitalization. • We call for more work on digitalization in transition studies. • We offer a number of perspectives to study the roles of digitalization in sustainability transitions.
