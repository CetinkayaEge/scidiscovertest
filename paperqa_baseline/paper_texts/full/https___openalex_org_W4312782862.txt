Title: Insights into sustainable aerogels from lignocellulosic materials
Authors: Hoang S. H. Nguyen, Huỳnh Kỳ Phương Hạ, Son Truong Nguyen, Van Thi Nguyen, Tuan Anh Nguyen, Anh N. Phan
Year: 2022
Venue: Journal of Materials Chemistry A
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2ta04994e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4312782862

Abstract:
Aerogels have been considered to be revolutionary solid-state materials due to their highly porous structure, low density, large surface area, and low thermal conductivity, which can be applied in the many modern fields of industry.
