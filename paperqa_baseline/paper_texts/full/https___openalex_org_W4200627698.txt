Title: Activating biophilic design patterns as a sustainable landscape approach
Authors: Sahar Ismail Mohamed Abdel Hady
Year: 2021
Venue: Journal of Engineering and Applied Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s44147-021-00031-x
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4200627698

Abstract:
Abstract Biophilic design elements are found around us in many landscape elements while we do not perceive them as biophilic design patterns. By developing our understanding of biophilic design as a phenomenon, we could discover simple ways to utilize landscape elements and transform them into a good biophilic design that might have positive impacts on a user’s health and well-being. Activating existing biophilic elements as an approach to a sustainable landscape has not been studied yet. Therefore, we rather analyse some international case studies in order to understand how biophilic design patterns can be implemented and see their different forms. Later, we will also go through an Egyptian biophilic design pattern case study and implement it to reach a sustainable landscape model. To summarize, the purpose of this study is to present a new sustainable landscape approach by activating biophilic design patterns in order to increase landscape efficiency.
