Title: Invasive alien plants in China: An update
Authors: Qiang Hao, Jin-Shuang Ma
Year: 2023
Venue: Plant Diversity
DOI: 10.1016/j.pld.2022.11.004
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9975470

Abstract:
Highlights • The latest 403 invasive alien plants in China are updated. • A summary of five volumes of ‘Alien Invasive Flora of China’ and recent invasive plants reports. • Compared with weeds, invasive plants emphasis on the destruction of biodiversity and native ecosystems. • Classical plant taxonomy is the basis of invasive plants research.
