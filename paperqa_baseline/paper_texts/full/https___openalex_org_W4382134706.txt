Title: Cellulose processing in ionic liquids from a materials science perspective: turning a versatile biopolymer into the cornerstone of our sustainable future
Authors: László Szabó, Romain Milotskyi, Gyanendra Sharma, Kenji Takahashi
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2gc04730f
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4382134706

Abstract:
The past two decades have seen fruitful efforts in shaping cellulose into functional materials using ionic liquids. This Tutorial Review aims at providing guidance from a materials science perspective to stimulate more research in this field.
