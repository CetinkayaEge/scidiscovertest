Title: A more sustainable synthesis approach for cellulose acetate using the DBU/CO2 switchable solvent system
Authors: Jonas Wolfs, Michaël A. R. Meier
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc01508g
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3163334256

Abstract:
Cellulose acetate was synthesized applying the DBU/CO2 switchable solvent system and a benign acetylating agent under mild conditions. High reagent conversion, a tunable degree of substitution and low cellulose backbone degradation were achieved.
