Title: Approaching a high-rate and sustainable production of hydrogen peroxide: oxygen reduction on Co–N–C single-atom electrocatalysts in simulated seawater
Authors: Qinglan Zhao, Yian Wang, Wei‐Hong Lai, Fei Xiao, Yuxiang Lyu, Caizhi Liao, Minhua Shao
Year: 2021
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1ee00878a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3196156321

Abstract:
Sustainable production of H 2 O 2 is boosted by oxygen reduction reaction on Co–N 5 sites in a flow cell in simulated seawater.
