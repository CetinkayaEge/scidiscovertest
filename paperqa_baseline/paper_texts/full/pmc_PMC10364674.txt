Title: Inferior mesenteric artery embolization ahead of rectal cancer surgery: AMIREMBOL pilot study
Authors: Julien Frandon, Laure Berny, Michel Prudhomme, Hélène de Forges, Chris Serrand, Fabien de Oliveira, Jean Paul Beregi, Martin M Bertrand
Year: 2022
Venue: The British Journal of Surgery
DOI: 10.1093/bjs/znac071
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10364674

Abstract:
Ischaemic conditioning in rectal cancer surgery by preoperative embolization of the inferior mesenteric artery is feasible and safe. It seems to protect from ischaemic stress during the surgical procedure.
