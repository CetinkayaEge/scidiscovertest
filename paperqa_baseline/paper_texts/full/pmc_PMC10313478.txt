Title: A Rare Mechanism for Spontaneous Closure of Muscular Ventricular Septal Defect in Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy
Authors: Martin R. Grübler, Alessia Azzu, Raad Mohiaddin
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101871
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313478

Abstract:
In the context of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM), a ventricular septal defect (VSD) is a rare finding. We present the case of a large spontaneously closed muscular VSD in a patient with HCM. We describe the role of cardiovascular magnetic resonance in the assessment of a VSD and its differential diagnosis in HCM. ( Level of Difficulty: Advanced. ) Central Illustration
