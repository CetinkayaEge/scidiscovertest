Title: Transitioning of petroleum-based plastic food packaging to sustainable bio-based alternatives
Authors: Yue Yin, Meng Wai Woo
Year: 2024
Venue: Sustainable Food Technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d4fb00028e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4392406130

Abstract:
Motivated by the urgent need to address environmental concerns associated with traditional food packaging, this review explores the shift towards sustainable, bio-based packaging solutions.
