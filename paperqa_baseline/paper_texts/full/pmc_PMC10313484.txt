Title: Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement Guided by Preprocedural Simulation of Fluoroscopic Location of the Membranous Septum
Authors: Shumpei Mori, Olcay Aksoy, Duc H. Do, Ravi H. Dave, Kalyanam Shivkumar
Year: 2023
Venue: JACC Case Reports
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2023.101888
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10313484

Abstract:
We show the virtual simulation of the fluoroscopic location of the membranous septum using preprocedural cardiac computed tomographic data sets. Recognizing the risk distance before the procedure can help individualize implantation strategy to reduce the risk of atrioventricular conduction axis damage during transcatheter aortic valve replacement.
