Title: Fluorescent carbon dots from birch leaves for sustainable electroluminescent devices
Authors: Shi Tang, Yongfeng Liu, Henry Opoku, Märta Gregorsson, Peijuan Zhang, Etienne Auroux, Dongfeng Dang, Anja‐Verena Mudring, Thomas Wågberg, Ludvig Edman, Jia Wang
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc03827k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4388144750

Abstract:
A mild solvothermal synthesis of carbon dots (CDs) from birch leaves is reported. The CDs feature a high quantum yield of 26% in ethanol, and deliver deep-red, narrowband emission ( λ peak = 675 nm, FWHM = 29 nm) in a light-emitting device.
