Title: An unusual case of vaccine-associated paralytic poliomyelitis
Authors: S Desai, T Diener, BJ-K Tan, NJ Lowry, C Talukdar, WM Chrusch, S Wiebe
Year: 2014
Venue: The Canadian Journal of Infectious Diseases & Medical Microbiology
DOI: 10.1155/2014/378320
Paper ID: pmc_PMC4173946

Abstract:
The present article reports a case involving an immunocompetent, previously well child who, despite two previous doses of inactivated poliovirus vaccine, developed severe flaccid paralysis consistent with polio after receiving oral polio vaccine.
