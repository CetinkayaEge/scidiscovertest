Title: Sustainability in peptide chemistry: current synthesis and purification technologies and future challenges
Authors: Lucia Ferrazzano, Martina Catani, Alberto Cavazzini, Giulia Martelli, Dario Corbisiero, Paolo Cantelmi, Tommaso Fantoni, Alexia Mattellone, Chiara De Luca, Simona Felletti, Walter Cabri, Alessandra Tolomelli
Year: 2022
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc04387k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4205136423

Abstract:
Developing greener synthesis processes is an inescapable necessity to transform the industrial landscape, mainly in the pharmaceutical sector, into a long-term, sustainable reality.
