Title: Zeolites in catalysis: sustainable synthesis and its impact on properties and applications
Authors: Hongwei Zhang, Ismail Bin Samsudin, Stephan Jaenicke, Gaik‐Khuan Chuah
Year: 2022
Venue: Catalysis Science & Technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2cy01325h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4293530485

Abstract:
Sustainable routes for the synthesis of heteroatom zeolites are highly sought after. Their synthesis, properties and catalytic performance are holistically discussed in this mini-review.
