Title: Live Combined Bacillus subtilis and Enterococcus faecium Ameliorate Murine Experimental Colitis by Immunosuppression
Authors: S. Chen, Y. Fu, L. L. Liu, W. Gao, Y. L. Liu, S. H. Fei, Y. Tan, K. F. Zou
Year: 2014
Venue: International Journal of Inflammation
DOI: 10.1155/2014/878054
Paper ID: pmc_PMC4170745

Abstract:
Live combined Bacillus subtilis and Enterococcus faecium ameliorate murine experimental colitis by immunosuppression manifested by downregulation of TLRs, macrophages, Th1, and Th2 but upregulation of Tregs.
