Title: Smart and Sustainable Planning for Cities and Regions
Authors: Adriano Bisello, Daniele Vettorato, David Ludlow, Claudia Baranzelli
Year: 2021
Venue: Green energy and technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-57764-3
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3133592871

Abstract:
This open access book gathers papers and case studies presented at the 3rd international conference Smart and Sustainable Planning for Cities and Regions (SSPCR), held in December 2019 in Bolzano, Italy, and explores the concept of smart and sustainable planning.
