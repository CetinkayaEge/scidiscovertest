Title: Managing Volunteers: Creating and Sustaining Impactful Programs
Authors: Anna Neya Kazanskaia
Year: 2024
Venue: 
DOI: https://doi.org/10.64357/managing-volunteers-2025
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4413024486

Abstract:
Managing Volunteers: Creating and Sustaining Impactful Programs provides a comprehensive guide to building volunteer initiatives that are both effective and sustainable. Dr. Anna Neya Kazanskaia explores the unique dynamics of volunteer management, from recruitment and training to retention and impact measurement. The book emphasizes practical strategies for creating meaningful, resilient programs that foster long-term social change while supporting the growth and motivation of volunteers.
