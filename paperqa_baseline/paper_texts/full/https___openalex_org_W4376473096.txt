Title: Renewable lignin and its macromolecule derivatives: an emerging platform toward sustainable electrochemical energy storage
Authors: Xueru Yang, Yufei Zhang, Minghui Ye, Yongchao Tang, Zhipeng Wen, Xiaoqing Liu, Cheng Chao Li
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc00565h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4376473096

Abstract:
This review overviews recent advances of the utilization of lignin-based/-derived macromolecules as key components of electrochemical energy storage devices. An outlook on future directions in this rapidly developing field is also envisioned.
