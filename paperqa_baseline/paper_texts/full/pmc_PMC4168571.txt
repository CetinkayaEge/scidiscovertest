Title: Making the most of food aid to help prevent child and maternal deaths
Authors: Bruce Cogill
Year: 2013
Venue: Global Health, Science and Practice
DOI: 10.9745/GHSP-D-13-00084
Paper ID: pmc_PMC4168571

Abstract:
Advances in child nutrition over the last several decades are creating momentum for a programmatic push to reduce undernutrition worldwide. The contribution of food aid may be small, but, nonetheless, U.S. food aid policy should be revamped to benefit more effectively and more efficiently the children and mothers in need.
