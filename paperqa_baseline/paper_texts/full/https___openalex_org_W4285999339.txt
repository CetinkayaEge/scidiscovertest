Title: The Sustainable Development Goals as a Transformative Force?
Authors: Frank Biermann, Thomas Hickmann, Carole‐Anne Sénit, Leonie Grob
Year: 2022
Venue: Cambridge University Press eBooks
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009082945.009
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4285999339

Abstract:
The scope of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and its 17 Sustainable Development Goals is unprecedented. So is the broad ambition expressed in this agreement. While the United Nations has rarely shied away from declaring farreaching policy aspirations in the past, the 2030 Agenda sets the bar highpossibly higher than it has ever been before, with ambitious goals to end hunger and poverty while simultaneously reducing unsustainable consumption and protecting the natural foundations of life on earth. The Sustainable Development Goals also add new areas of policy ambition that have not been promoted in this form before, such as reducing global and national inequalities or promoting good governance, both now enshrined as stand-alone global policy goals.
