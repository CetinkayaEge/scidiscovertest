Title: Polybenzoxazines: a sustainable platform for the design of fast responsive and catalyst-free vitrimers based on trans-esterification exchanges
Authors: Antoine Adjaoud, Acerina Trejo-Machín, Laura Puchot, Pierre Verge
Year: 2021
Venue: Polymer Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1py00324k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3160069208

Abstract:
This work explores a new strategy, aiming for the synthesis of catalyst-free vitrimers by taking advantage of the abundant number of tertiary amines covalently bound into a polybenzoxazine network.
