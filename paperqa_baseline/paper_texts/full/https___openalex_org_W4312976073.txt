Title: Quantitative sustainable design (QSD) for the prioritization of research, development, and deployment of technologies: a tutorial and review
Authors: Yalin Li, John T. Trimmer, Steven Hand, Xinyi Zhang, Katherine Chambers, Hannah A. C. Lohman, Rui Shi, Diana M. Byrne, Sherri M. Cook, Jeremy S. Guest
Year: 2022
Venue: Environmental Science Water Research & Technology
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2ew00431c
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4312976073

Abstract:
This tutorial review synthesizes literature on sustainability analyses to introduce quantitative sustainable design (QSD) for technology research, development, and deployment.
