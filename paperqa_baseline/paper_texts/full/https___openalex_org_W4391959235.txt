Title: Effective green treatment of sewage sludge from Fenton reactions: Utilizing MoS 2 for sustainable resource recovery

Year: 2024
Venue: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2317394121
