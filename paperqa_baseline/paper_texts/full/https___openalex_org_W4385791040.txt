Title: Tackling antimicrobial resistance by integrating One Health and the Sustainable Development Goals
Authors: Angeline Ferdinand, Mauricio J. C. Coppo, Benjamin P. Howden, Glenn F. Browning
Year: 2023
Venue: BMC Global and Public Health
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s44263-023-00003-8
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4385791040

Abstract:
Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) has been identified as a leading threat to global public health. One Health approaches that integrate sectors across human health, animal health, food production and the environment are essential to both addressing the growing threat of AMR and achieving the Sustainable Development Goals.
