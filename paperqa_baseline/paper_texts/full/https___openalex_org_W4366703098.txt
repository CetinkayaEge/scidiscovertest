Title: Bio-based agricultural products: a sustainable alternative to agrochemicals for promoting a circular economy
Authors: Anisha Priya, Avinash Alagumalai, D. Balaji, Hua Song
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3su00075c
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4366703098

Abstract:
Utilizing waste streams to produce bio-based products has the potential to promote a circular economy. In addition, by incorporating biowaste into the circular economy, the production of sustainable bioproducts and bioenergy can be enhanced.
