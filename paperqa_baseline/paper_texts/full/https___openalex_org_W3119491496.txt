Title: Sustainable, self-cleaning, transparent, and moisture/oxygen-barrier coating films for food packaging
Authors: Vu Thi Thuy, Lâm Tấn Hào, Hyeonyeol Jeon, Jun Mo Koo, Jaeduk Park, Jaeduk Park, Eun Seong Lee, Sung Yeon Hwang, Sejin Choi, Jeyoung Park, Jeyoung Park, Dongyeop X. Oh
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d0gc03647a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3119491496

Abstract:
Food packaging requirements of gas barrier and self-cleaning are satisfied with the use of bio-renewable cellulose, chitosan, silica, and sunflower oil.
