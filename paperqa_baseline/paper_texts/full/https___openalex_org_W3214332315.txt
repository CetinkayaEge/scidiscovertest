Title: From green to blue economy: Marine biorefineries for a sustainable ocean-based economy
Authors: Nathalia Vieira Porphirio Veríssimo, Cassamo U. Mussagy, Ariane Alves Oshiro, Carlos Miguel Nóbrega Mendonça, Valéria C. Santos‐Ebinuma, Adalberto Pessoa, Ricardo Pinheiro de Souza Oliveira, Jorge F. B. Pereira
Year: 2021
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d1gc03191k
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3214332315

Abstract:
Biorefinery is the ideal model to help marine industries to apply green and blue economy principles towards a more sustainable, profitable, and conscious ocean economy.
