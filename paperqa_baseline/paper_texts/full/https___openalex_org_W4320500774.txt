Title: High performance, recyclable and sustainable by design natural polyphenol-based epoxy polyester thermosets
Authors: Roxana Dinu, Anastasiia Pidvoronia, Ugo Lafont, Olivier Damiano, Alice Mija
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2gc04414e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4320500774

Abstract:
Development of high-performance materials with a high content of aromatic units and high functionality, using natural and renewable polyphenolic synthons such as naringenin and phloroglucinol.
