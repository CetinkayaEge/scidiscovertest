Title: A review of the green chemistry approaches to leather tanning in imparting sustainable leather manufacturing
Authors: Mohammad Mahbubul Hassan, Jane Gary Harris, James J. C. Busfield, Emiliano Bilotti
Year: 2023
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc02948d
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4385980919

Abstract:
Green chemistry approaches studied for leather tanning are critically reviewed. The tanning performance and mechanical properties are discussed. The mechanisms of tanning for various tanning agents and future directions are outlined.
