Title: From fossil to green chemicals: sustainable pathways and new carbon feedstocks for the global chemical industry
Authors: Gabriel Lopez, Dominik Keiner, Mahdi Fasihi, Tuomas Koiranen, Christian Breyer
Year: 2023
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3ee00478c
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4377080158

Abstract:
Green methanol and green ammonia produced by renewable electricity and electricity-based hydrogen become the backbone for a defossilised global chemical industry.
