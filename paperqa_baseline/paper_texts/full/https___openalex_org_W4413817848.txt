Title: Strategies for Sustainable Growth
Authors: Neya Global, Anna Neya Kazanskaia
Year: 2025
Venue: 
DOI: https://doi.org/10.64357/strategies-sustainable-growth-2025
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4413817848

Abstract:
Sustainable growth in non-profit organizations requires balancing mission impact with financial stability and organizational capacity. This book outlines strategies for achieving long-term growth, including diversification of funding, capacity building, stakeholder engagement, and adaptive planning. Case studies and practical tools provide guidance for leaders seeking to expand their organization’s reach and resilience.
