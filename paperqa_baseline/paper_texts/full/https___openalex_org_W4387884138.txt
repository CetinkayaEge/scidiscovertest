Title: An actionable definition and criteria for “sustainable chemistry” based on literature review and a global multisectoral stakeholder working group
Authors: Amy Cannon, Sally Edwards, Molly Jacobs, Jonathon Moir, Monika A. Roy, Joel Tickner
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3su00217a
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4387884138

Abstract:
Sustainable chemistry is the development and application of chemicals, chemical processes, and products that benefit current and future generations without harmful impacts to humans or ecosystems.
