From thermodynamic analysis, the derived parameters of ΔG (-11.6 ∼ -6.2 kJ/mol), ΔH and ΔS demonstrate a spontaneous, enthalpy controlled adsorption process that was exothermic for RB (-10.0 kJ/mol) and PC (-23.9 kJ/mol) and endothermic for PY (3.9 kJ/mol) and PN (13.2 kJ/mol).
