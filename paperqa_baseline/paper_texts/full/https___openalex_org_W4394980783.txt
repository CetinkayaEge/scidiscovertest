Title: Phytoremediation as a green and sustainable prospective method for heavy metal contamination: a review
Authors: Md. Merajul Islam, Neha Saxena, Deepa Sharma
Year: 2024
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3su00440f
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4394980783

Abstract:
Cleaning up heavy metal-polluted soil through various mechanisms of phytoremediation is an economical, aesthetically beautiful, and environmentally responsible alternative to chemical or physicochemical approaches for heavy metal decontamination.
