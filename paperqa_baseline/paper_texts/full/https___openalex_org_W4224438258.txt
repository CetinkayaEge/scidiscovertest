Title: It Takes Time: Vigilance and Sustained Attention Assessment in Adults with ADHD
Authors: Anselm B. M. Fuermaier, Lara Tucha, Nana Guo, Christian Mette, Bernhard Müller, Norbert Scherbaum, Oliver Tucha
Year: 2022
Venue: International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19095216
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4224438258

Abstract:
: This study underscores the role of vigilance and sustained attention tests in the assessment of adult ADHD. It is further concluded that summary scores over the entire test duration are sufficient, but that all variables of a test should be considered. Finally, we hypothesize that reassessment on a different day may benefit a more accurate clinical assessment of adults with ADHD, in order to adequately take intraindividual fluctuations and limitations regarding test reliability into account.
