Title: COVID-19 Testing to Sustain In-Person Instruction and Extracurricular Activities in High Schools — Utah, November 2020–March 2021
Authors: William A. Lanier, Kendra D. Babitz, Abigail Collingwood, Maggie F. Graul, Sydnee Dickson, L. Troy Cunningham, Angela C. Dunn, Duncan MacKellar, Adam L. Hersh
Year: 2021
Venue: MMWR Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report
DOI: https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm7021e2
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3161066521

Abstract:
School-based COVID-19 testing should be considered as part of a comprehensive prevention strategy to help identify SARS-CoV-2 infections in schools and sustain in-person instruction and extracurricular activities.
