Title: With a little help from my T friends: T cells increase efficacy of KRAS (G12D) inhibitors
Authors: Sara Mainardi
Year: 2023
Venue: Cell Reports Medicine
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.100950
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9975326

Abstract:
Mutation-selective KRAS inhibitors are revolutionizing the treatment of RAS-mutant tumors but cannot achieve durable effects alone. Kemp and colleagues 1 recently showed how the KRAS-G12D-specific inhibitor MRTX1133, while impairing cancer proliferation, stimulates T cell infiltration, which is crucial for sustained disease control.
