Title: Plant synthetic biology: exploring the frontiers of sustainable agriculture and fundamental plant biology
Authors: Jae‐Seong Yang, Ivan Reyna‐Llorens
Year: 2023
Venue: Journal of Experimental Botany
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/jxb/erad220
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4384625923

Abstract:
Plant synthetic biology allows for the reprogramming of biological systems to promote sustainable agriculture. It has a range of applications, including improving nutritional quality, engineering resilient crops, creating new materials, and producing various bioproducts. In addition, plant synthetic biology provides a new way to answer fundamental questions in plant biology. This Special Issue presents a collection of review articles that delve into the current and future state of plant synthetic biology.
