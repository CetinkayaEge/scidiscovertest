Title: Evaluation of green chemistry metrics for sustainable recycling of platinum group metals from spent automotive catalysts via bioleaching
Authors: Salman Karim, Han Mei Saw, Yen‐Peng Ting
Year: 2024
Venue: Green Chemistry
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3gc03918h
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4392522735

Abstract:
This work evaluates the sustainability indicators of the biorecovery of platinum group metals from spent automotive catalysts with due consideration of the environment and efficiency for the evaluation of environmental sustainability.
