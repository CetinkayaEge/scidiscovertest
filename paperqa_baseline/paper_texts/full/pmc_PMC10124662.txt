Title: Presence of Burkholderia pseudomallei in Soil, Nigeria, 2019
Authors: Jelmer Savelkoel, Rita O. Oladele, Chiedozie K. Ojide, Rebecca F. Peters, Daan W. Notermans, Justina O. Makinwa, Maaike C. de Vries, Marion A.E. Sunter, Sébastien Matamoros, Nasiru Abdullahi, Uche S. Unigwe, Alani S. Akanmu, W. Joost Wiersinga, Emma Birnie
Year: 2023
Venue: Emerging Infectious Diseases
DOI: 10.3201/eid2905.221138
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10124662

Abstract:
Melioidosis, caused by the soil-dwelling bacterium Burkholderia pseudomallei , is predicted to be endemic in Nigeria but is only occasionally reported. This report documents the systematic identification of the presence of B. pseudomallei and B. thailandensis in the soil across multiple states in Nigeria.
