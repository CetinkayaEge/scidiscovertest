Title: A tour de force provides novel insights into CD19-CAR T cell resistance mechanisms
Authors: Aimee C. Talleur, Stephen Gottschalk
Year: 2023
Venue: Cell Reports Medicine
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2023.100949
Paper ID: pmc_PMC9975327

Abstract:
Sworder et al. 1 developed an integrated simultaneous tumor and effector profiling (STEP) approach to study resistance mechanisms to CD19-CAR T cell therapy in large B-cell lymphomas. Their study provides novel biological insights and paves the way for future interventions.
