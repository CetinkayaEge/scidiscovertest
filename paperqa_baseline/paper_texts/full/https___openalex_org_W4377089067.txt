Title: Are you ready for the sustainable, biocircular economy?
Authors: Donna Marshall, Aideen O’Dochartaigh, Andrea Prothero, Orlagh Reynolds, Enrico Secchi
Year: 2023
Venue: Business Horizons
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bushor.2023.05.002
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4377089067

Abstract:
With the effects of the climate crisis becoming more extreme and the need to rapidly achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs); managers, companies, and entire industries need to embrace the sustainable bio-circular economy, enabling all stakeholders, alongside our planet to thrive in this inevitable new future. This paper clarifies the emerging concept of sustainable bio-circularity by showcasing best practice applications with examples from policymakers, Civil Society Organizations (CSOs), companies, and others working together to bring vital transformational change. We present the guiding EARTH principles which will help ensure a sustainable bio-circular economy transition that benefits organizations and society, without negatively impacting our natural environment. We next introduce the five stages required to develop a successful transition to sustainable bio-circularity. Here we highlight how an integrated systems-based STOP roadmap for organizations can enable them to conduct strategic analysis and decision making across each of the five stages, thus helping to achieve sustainable bio-circular transition. We focus specifically on Skills, Technologies, Opportunities, and Problems. We demonstrate how the guiding principles, the five stages, and the road map are intertwined and stress how companies must understand and embrace each in order to thrive in this new environment. Finally, we provide a bio-washing checklist to help ensure this transition is truly sustainable, just and authentic.
