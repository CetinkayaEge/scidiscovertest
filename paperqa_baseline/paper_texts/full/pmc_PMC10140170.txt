Title: Tissue clearing and three-dimensional imaging of the whole cochlea and vestibular system from multiple large-animal models
Authors: Adele Moatti, Yuheng Cai, Chen Li, Kristen D. Popowski, Ke Cheng, Frances S. Ligler, Alon Greenbaum
Year: 2023
Venue: STAR Protocols
DOI: 10.1016/j.xpro.2023.102220
Paper ID: pmc_PMC10140170

Abstract:
Summary The inner ear of humans and large animals is embedded in a thick and dense bone that makes dissection challenging. Here, we present a protocol that enables three-dimensional (3D) characterization of intact inner ears from large-animal models. We describe steps for decalcifying bone, using solvents to remove color and lipids, and imaging tissues in 3D using confocal and light sheet microscopy. We then detail a pipeline to count hair cells in antibody-stained and 3D imaged cochleae using open-source software.
