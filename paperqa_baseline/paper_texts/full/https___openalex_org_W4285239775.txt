Title: SUSTAINABILITY AND THE ROLE OF HRM
Authors: Teresa Proença
Year: 2022
Venue: Revista de Administração de Empresas
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1590/s0034-759020220513
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4285239775

Abstract:
Sustainability is not a new subject. Both society and business are increasingly becoming aware of the importance of a sustainable development "that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs" (World Commission on Environment and Development, 1987, p. 8). However, managers still need to comprehend how to deal with the challenges of achieving sustainability
