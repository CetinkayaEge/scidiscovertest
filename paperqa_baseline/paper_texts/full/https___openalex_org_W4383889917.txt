Title: Sustainable design of water–energy–food nexus: a literature review
Authors: Juan Gabriel Segovia‐Hernández, Gabriel Contreras‐Zarazúa, César Ramírez‐Márquez
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d3su00110e
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4383889917

Abstract:
The water–energy–food nexus aims to achieve sustainable development by meeting present needs while safeguarding the capacity of future generations.
