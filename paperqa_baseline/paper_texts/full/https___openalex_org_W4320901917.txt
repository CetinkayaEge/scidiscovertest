Title: Sustainable materials for renewable energy storage in the thermal battery
Authors: Samantha L. Piper, Craig M. Forsyth, Mega Kar, Callum Gassner, R. Vijayaraghavan, Surianarayanan Mahadevan, Karolina Matuszek, Jennifer M. Pringle, Douglas R. MacFarlane
Year: 2023
Venue: RSC Sustainability
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2su00111j
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4320901917

Abstract:
This work demonstrates the feasibility of aliphatic dicarbamate PCMs as inexpensive, stable and sustainable materials for the storage of renewable energy in the “Thermal Battery”.
