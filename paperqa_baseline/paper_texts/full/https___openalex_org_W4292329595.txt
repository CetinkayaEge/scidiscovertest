Title: Vapor and heat dual-drive sustainable power for portable electronics in ambient environments
Authors: Jiaxin Bai, Yajie Hu, Tianlei Guang, Kaixuan Zhu, Haiyan Wang, Huhu Cheng, Feng Liu, Liangti Qu
Year: 2022
Venue: Energy & Environmental Science
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/d2ee00846g
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W4292329595

Abstract:
A highly efficient vapor and heat dual-drive electric generator provides a high short-circuit current density and can work as a personal energy supply system to support wearable devices under real, natural scenarios in different seasons.
