Title: The Drivers of Environmentally Sustainable Hospital Foodservices
Authors: Stefanie Carino, Shirin Malekpour, Judi Porter, Jorja Collins
Year: 2021
Venue: Frontiers in Nutrition
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2021.740376
Paper ID: https://openalex.org/W3207520233

Abstract:
These findings examine the successful international cases of sustainable hospital foodservices to provide a global overview to assist with strategic planning both within hospitals and within governing bodies.
